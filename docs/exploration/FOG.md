# Fog

Fog weather in star systems: the roll, the fog terrain and its sensor cut, the fluid solver that moves the fog, the shader that draws it, and the lurker fleets that ambush inside it. Read it before touching `indevo/exploration/fog/` or `IndEvo_fog_weather`. It does not cover the Chaff Cloud consumable ([Consumables](../items/CONSUMABLES.md)), which keeps its own terrain and renderer.

Checked against Starsector 0.98a-RC8 (knowledge skill build 0.98.5a-RC4) and IE `55b1c98`. Recheck engine facts after a game update. Not yet seen in game.

## Overview

On each economy tick `FogWeatherManager` rolls every fog capable star system in the sector, so the amount of fog scales with the sector size. A system holds one spell or up to 2 banks. Banks are common (about one bank per two systems), spells rarer (about one system in 14). The sector is seeded once at the start, so fog is spread out from day one. Fog comes in two kinds:

| Kind | Size and place | Timing | Lurkers | Cache |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spell | An oversized bank centred on the star (the system centre without one): same lobe shape, outer reach = the system radius (farthest planet or jump point + 2500, at least 8000) held between `SPELL_MIN_RADIUS` 9000 and `SPELL_MAX_RADIUS` 20000. The body covers about 15 to 30% of the system disc, never the whole system | Rolls out from the star over 2 days, holds 25..35 days, fades over 8 days | `SPELL_AMBUSH_CHANCE` 35%: 1 or 2 fleets, else none | Never |
| Bank | 8000..14000 across (outer reach), a misshapen cluster of bent lobes. 60% wrapped round a gas giant and moving with it, else on a slow orbit round the star between planet orbits | Thickens in place over 2 days, holds 90..180 days, fades over 8 days | `BANK_AMBUSH_CHANCE` 10%: 1 fleet, else none | `BANK_CACHE_CHANCE` 15%: one small vanilla cache hidden inside, removed with the bank |

Inside fog the sensor range of a fleet and the range at which it is detected both drop to `1 - 0.9 * density`, so two fleets in thick fog only see each other when they almost touch. With `IndEvo_fog_sensorContacts` on, the player sees fleets further out as unidentified sensor contacts (Sensor contacts). Moving fleets, planets and stations push the fog, fleets leave trails, Emergency Burn blows a hole and Interdiction Pulse an expanding ring. Artillery shots and missiles cut it too and leave heat or an ion charge in it ([Shot effects](#shot-effects)).

## Registration and lifecycle

- `ModPlugin.onGameLoad() -> setListenersIfNeeded() -> FogWeatherManager.register()` adds a transient listener if none is there and seeds the sector (`seedIfNeeded`) unless `IndEvo_fogSeeded` is set or `IndEvo_fog_weather` is `Off`; with `Off` the seed runs on the first tick after it is turned on. Old saves without the flag get the seed on their next load.
- Fog is a terrain entity added with `addTerrain`, saved with its plugin. It orbits the system centre or a gas giant (`setCircularOrbit(focus, 0, 0, 100)` like vanilla magnetic fields, or a star orbit with period `0.1 * radius` days).
- Lurkers are fleets with a saved `FogLurkerAssignmentAI` script.
- A bank's cache is a vanilla salvage entity on an orbit round the fog entity; the plugin keeps it in the saved field `cache` and expires it when the fog is over.
- The grid, movers, rings, clear patches and the keep discs are transient fields of the plugin, built while the player is in the system and dropped when the player leaves. GL objects are static in `FogRenderer`, created once per application.

`reportEconomyTick -> [IndEvo_fog_weather not Off] seedIfNeeded -> getActiveFog (prune) -> per system: isCapable -> isSpellReady + hasRoom -> spawn -> pickBankSpot + addTerrain -> spawnCache -> spawnLurker -> FogTerrainPlugin.advance -> BaseTerrain.advance (applyEffect) -> advanceView -> FogGrid.step -> render -> FogRenderer.render`

## Flow

### Roll

- `reportEconomyTick` (`FogWeatherManager.java:96`): returns while `IndEvo_fog_weather` is `Off`, runs `seedIfNeeded`, then rolls each system that passes `isCapable`. Each tick (10 per month, `economyIterPerMonth`): `SPELL_CHANCE` 0.4% when `isSpellReady` and the system has no fog, else `BANK_CHANCE` 1% while the system has no spell and fewer than `MAX_BANKS_PER_SYSTEM` 2 banks. `MAX_BANKS` 300 is a safety net only; fog outside the player's system costs nothing (`FogTerrainPlugin.advance` returns early). With banks living about 145 days that is about one bank per two systems, a new bank in a given system about every 10 months, and a spell in about one system in 14.
- Spell cooldown: `spawn` writes `$IndEvo_fogSpellStart` and `$IndEvo_fogSpellWait` (roll in + hold + fade + `SPELL_COOLDOWN_DAYS` 240) to the system memory, so a system gets no spell for 8 months after one ends. Banks have no cooldown.
- `seedIfNeeded` (`:119`): once per save, each capable system rolls the steady state: a spell at `SPELL_CHANCE x mean spell life / tick days`, else 2 bank slots each at `BANK_CHANCE x mean bank life / tick days / 2` (about 24%). Seeded fog has no roll in and a hold shortened by a random share, so expiries are staggered.
- `isFogCapable(system)` (`:186`): not `THEME_HIDDEN`, `THEME_SPECIAL`, `SYSTEM_ABYSSAL`, `SYSTEM_CUT_OFF_FROM_HYPER`, and nebula cover at most `MAX_NEBULA_COVER` 10%. Core systems get spells and banks. The manager caches the result per system (`isCapable`) because tags and nebula do not change in play. `getNebulaCover` counts the tiles of every `nebula` terrain (`BaseTiledTerrain.getTiles`, 400 units each) whose centre lies in the system disc (`getSystemRadius`: outermost planet or jump point plus 2500, at least 8000) against that disc's area, so nebula in the outskirts does not count. Systems under the limit take the nebula's colour. Markets do not block fog.
- `hasRoom(loc, kind)` (`:172`): a spell needs a system without fog, a bank a system without a spell and with fewer than 2 banks. `spawn` (`:225`) throws without room.
- `pickBankSpot` (`:272`): up to 10 tries of a random gas giant with 60%, else a star orbit at the midpoint between two adjacent planet orbits or beyond the outermost, at least star radius + bank radius + 500 out. A spot is taken only when its distance from the system centre differs from every other bank's by at least the sum of both radii, so two banks never touch while they orbit. No clear spot: no bank this tick.

### Strength and density

- Strength (`getStrength`, `:220`): a bank thickens in place, 0 to 1 over the roll in; a spell is at 1 from the start and rolls out behind a front instead. Both: 1 during hold, 1 to 0 over the fade, from `getElapsedDaysSince(start)`. At the end the terrain removes its entity and the manager drops it on the next tick.
- Spell roll in (`getFront`, `:228`): `Shape.front` is a distance from the centre that grows linearly from 0 to `radius * FRONT_REACH 1.3 + FogGrid.FRONT_WIDTH 1500` over the roll in, then `FogGrid.NO_FRONT`. `Shape.mask` cuts the body beyond it: the edge is `front * (0.78..1.22 noise)`, so it is ragged, and the fog rises over the 1500 units behind the edge. For a 14000 radius spell the edge moves about 985 units per second (20 s for the roll in). Every reader of the rest mask follows the front (sensor cut, sound, map outline). `getFullMaskAt` reads the mask without the front; lurker posts are picked with it, so a lurker stands in the open until the front has passed its post.
- Rest mask (`FogGrid.Shape.mask`): in bank local coordinates (position minus the terrain entity's location), time = elapsed days times seconds per day. Banks and spells share one shape (`bankBody`): 5..7 stretched lobes from the seed (`buildLobes`, lobe scale = radius / `Shape.LOBE_REACH` 2.4) in a slowly drifting warped space. The distance to the nearest lobe, in lobe sizes, is scaled by a slow outline noise (0.45..1.75) and the body is solid inside `EDGE_IN` 0.62 and gone at `EDGE_OUT` 1.3, so the middle of every lobe is full fog and only the outline is thin; ridged noise adds bent arms out to `ARM_REACH` 2.6 lobe sizes. Trails, the sensor cut and the sound need the solid body: do not thin it to shape the outline. The outline reaches about 1.1..1.3 radius. Times a 0.5..1.2 inner noise and the nebula gate (`Shape.gridMask`, what the grid holds), times the clear rings (`Shape.keepAt`; `Shape.mask` is the product; `computeKeep`, `:501`, `Shape.KEEP_STRIDE` 6 floats: x, y, inner, outer, hazeIn, hazeOut: no fog inside inner, full fog from outer). Planets and moons: surface to `PLANET_FADE` 2.3 radii, at least `MIN_FADE` 100 units wide. The rings are drawn per pixel, so a ring may be narrower than a grid cell. Stars (not nebula centres) get a wide space with a haze floor: in units of the star radius, held to `STAR_UNIT_MIN` 350..`STAR_UNIT_MAX` 550, above the surface a haze of `Shape.HAZE` 0.5 density rises from `STAR_HAZE_IN` 0.15 to `STAR_HAZE_OUT` 0.9 units, and the fog thickens from `STAR_GAP_IN` 2 to full at `STAR_GAP_OUT` 5.5 units (a 450 unit star: haze from 520, full fog from 2925; a 1000 unit star: full fog from 4025; a 180 unit star counts as 350: haze from 232, full fog from 2105). The floor keeps the lit haze of a small star outside its corona, which is at least 300 units wide (`coronaMin`, `star_gen_data.csv`). The haze is what the sunlight falls on. Stations (radius + `STATION_KEEP` 100) are kept clear in banks and spells, jump points (radius + `JUMP_POINT_KEEP` 150) and market entities (radius + 100) in banks only, from 1.05 to 1.7 of that. Rebuilt every 0.1 days; in view the rings follow their bodies every frame (Grid and drawing).
- No fog on nebula: `computeCovers` builds one `FogCover` per `nebula` terrain when the shape is made (tiles never change): tiles from `BaseTiledTerrain.getTiles()` (`[i][j]`, i along x, field centred on the terrain entity, knowledge skill `api/impl/campaign/terrain/BaseTiledTerrain.java:157`) on a half tile grid, box blurred three times, so cover is about 0.5 on the edge of a tile block and 0 a tile and a half out. `Shape.mask` multiplies by `1 - smooth(GATE_IN 0.03, GATE_OUT 0.4, cover)`: with 400 unit tiles the fog starts to thin about 500 units before a nebula tile and is gone about 80 units before it. Covers are in system coordinates, `Shape.anchorX/Y` (set on every `getShape`) carry the fog centre, so an orbiting bank passes nebula correctly. Every reader of the rest mask gets the gate: sensor cut, sound, map, cache and lurker spots.
- `getDensityAt` (`FogTerrainPlugin.java:239`): rest mask times strength, never the live grid. Stats, sound, the tooltip, `containsPoint` (density above 0.05), the cache and lurkers all read it. A fleet always sits in the hole it cuts, so the live density at a fleet is near 0 and must not drive any effect; trails and blown holes are visual only.

### Sensor cut

`applyEffect` (`:341`) adds `1 - 0.9 * density` as a 0.1 day temporary mult to `getDetectedRangeMod()` and `getSensorRangeMod()`, as the vanilla abyss does. While a fleet's Active Sensor Burst is active or in progress the sensor range mult is skipped; the detected range mult stays. `BaseTerrain.advance` applies one terrain per effect category per fleet per frame, so fog never stacks with itself.

### Sensor contacts

With `IndEvo_fog_sensorContacts` on (default off), the player sees a fleet that the sensor cut hides as a vanilla sensor contact (sparkles, no ships, no faction) while it is inside a lighter cut, `CONTACT_CUT` 0.5 instead of 0.9 per side. Identification stays at the cut range. With an open range of 1000 the fleet is a contact from 250 units when both fleets sit in thick fog and from 500 when only one does; ships and faction show from about 10 and 50 units as before. Engine facts checked against the 0.98.5a-RC7 jars.

- `FogTerrainPlugin.isFogContact(target, observer)` (`:260`): observer is the player fleet, target another fleet in the same location with a sensor profile, at least one fog stat mod on the two, setting on (read after the stat mods, so fleets outside fog never read it). Range is `observer.getMaxSensorRangeToDetect(target)` times the lift of each side (`getContactLift`, `:280`: the applied mult `m` gives the density `(1 - m) / 0.9`, lift is `(1 - 0.5 * density) / m`). The easy mode bonus is taken out before the lift and added back, then the engine cap (`sensorRangeMax`, 5000) applies, as in the engine (knowledge skill `obf/campaign/BaseCampaignEntity.java:1089`). True only beyond the vanilla range and inside the lifted one, edge to edge.
- `FogSensorContactPatch` hooks `isVisibleToSensorsOf(SectorEntityToken)` and `getVisibilityLevelTo(SectorEntityToken)` on every `SectorEntityToken` subtype; both are declared on the game's `BaseCampaignEntity` (inferred name, the method names are API) and fleets inherit them. A fog contact turns `false` into `true` and the level into `SENSOR_CONTACT`. The engine needs both: `SensorContactIndicatorManager.advance` shows an entity only when `isVisibleToPlayerFleet()` (the first method) is true, then draws the sparkles for `SENSOR_CONTACT` (`obf/campaign/SensorContactIndicatorManager.java:59`). Vanilla gives full details within 50 units (`detectionRangeDetailsAlwaysMin`) even where its sensor check hides the fleet; the hook makes those contacts too, unless the unpatched check shows the fleet (dev mode sensors off), so a fleet in thick fog is identified only inside the cut range, as before.
- The sparkles are drawn in `BaseLocation.renderIndicators`, after every layer (`obf/campaign/CampaignEngine.java:795`), so they show over the fog. The fleet sprite needs `COMPOSITION_DETAILS`, so a contact draws no ships.
- Only the player's view changes. NPC detection, lurkers and the sensor cut are as before. Everything vanilla does with a sensor contact applies: the new contact ping, the lost sight sound, Interdiction Pulse and detachments treat it as seen.
- `feedMovers` reads the unpatched level (`FogSensorContactPatch.getVanillaLevelToPlayerFleet`) for the per pixel fleet patch, so contacts cut no clear patch and the fog look is unchanged.

### Sound

`terrain.json` gives `IndEvo_fog` the vanilla nebula's sound: `musicSuppression` 1 and `loopOne` `terrain_nebula`. `BaseTerrain.advance` plays them while the player fleet is inside (density above 0.05). `getProximitySoundFactor` scales both by `getDensityAt` at the player, full at `FULL_SOUND_DENSITY` 0.6, so music fades out and the hum fades in with the fog's thickness.

### Grid and drawing

- `advanceView` (`:564`), only in the player's location and while the view centre is within the render range. 128 x 128 cells of 80 units in bank local coordinates, recentred by whole cells when the view centre drifts more than 8 cells; cells that scroll in start at the rest mask, border cells are held at the rest mask. One solver step per frame, `dt` clamped to `FogGrid.MAX_DT` (0.1 s). The frame `dt` is summed and the grid steps once the sum reaches `FogMode.minStep`: 0 (every frame) except `LOW` at 1/30 s, so at most 30 steps a second; movers, blasts, rings and clear patches still feed every frame. While a spell rolls in the grid relaxes to the rest mask with `FRONT_TAU` 1.5 s in place of `RELAX_TAU` (no fog is around yet that could flow in), and `FogGrid.emanate` sets an outward flow of `FRONT_PUSH` 320 units per second in the band behind the front, so the fog streams away from the star.
- Movers (`feedMovers`, `:575`): every fleet in the window with its velocity minus the bank's own velocity; planets and stations with their frame to frame speed (jumps over 600 units count as no motion), `BODY_RADIUS_MULT` 0.8 of their radius and `BODY_PAD` 20, so they cut 1.2 radii plus 20 units, inside their clear ring; meteor shower and rogue rocks (`MeteorSwarmManager.METEOR_TAG`) the same way, with `ROCK_RADIUS_MULT` 0.4 of their entity radius (the hit radius is twice the sprite), `ROCK_PAD` 10 and `ROCK_CUT` 0.8.
- Carve radius (`FogGrid.mover`): `Mover.r * 1.5 + Mover.pad`, pad 60 by default. A rock cuts 1.2 sprite radii plus 10 units: 22 units for a size 20 rock, 130 for size 200. The grid cannot hold a hole narrower than one cell (80 units): such a mover reaches one cell, and its cut, push and swirl are scaled by its share of the cell area (radius squared / cell squared), so a small rock only stirs the fog and leaves no lane. Its clear space is a clear patch (below).
- Body wakes (`FogGrid.wake`): a mover's cut scales with speed / 170, and a moon at about 15 units per second cannot outrun the refill. Planets and stations are fed with `Mover.body`: full cut at any speed above 2, plus a tapering cut at up to `WAKE_SAMPLES` 12 points kept along the path behind, spaced half a carve radius and scaled by the same area share. About 480 units for a small moon. Rocks and fleets are fast enough and get none.
- Abilities (`readAbilities`, `:634`): Emergency Burn blasts when its level reaches 1; Interdiction Pulse rings out to `InterdictionPulseAbility.getRange` over 0.6 s when `$justDidInterdictionPulse` appears. Polled per fleet in the window: no callback reports NPC ability use, and the pulse's level is 1 for at most a frame (duration 0.001 days, `abilities.csv`), so the memory flag is the reliable signal.
- Shots and explosions (`feedShot`): one pass over `LocationAPI.getCustomEntities()` per frame, by plugin class. Polled: no callback reports a new custom entity. Any `ExplosionEntityPlugin` (vanilla `explosion`, `IndEvo_VariableExplosion`, `IndEvo_mine_explosion`, other mods' subclasses) blasts once when first seen in the window. The crater radius is the explosion radius (up to `MAX_BLAST_RADIUS` 1200) x `BLAST_SCALE` (0.6 / `WAVE`, about 0.55): clear inside half the crater radius, thinning out to 1.05 of it. Vanilla `params` has no getter, so the radius is `(getRenderRange() - entity radius) / 3`. Missile carriers (`CARRIER_CUT` 2), submunitions and consumable missiles (`BaseMissileEntityPlugin`) are movers with their frame to frame speed. Mortar shells fly above the fog and are skipped; only their explosion blasts it. Railgun lances, heat, charge and storms: [Shot effects](#shot-effects).
- Clear patches: the player fleet always, other fleets while the player can see them (fade in 0.5 s, out 0.8 s), radius 2.6 x fleet radius. Rocks in the window get one of `ROCK_PATCH_MULT` 0.8 entity radii: no fog inside 0.36 entity radii, full fog from 1.2. Up to `FogRenderer.MAX_HOLES` 48 in all; fleets are added first and the player fleet is never dropped.
- `render` draws on `TERRAIN_8` (above `FLEETS`) one world space quad over the visible part of the window. The texture is packed and uploaded only when `FogGrid.version` changed (one bump per solver step and per `recentre`) or the grid is a new one. Anything that moves the cell origin (`ox`, `oy`) must bump `version`: with `FogMode.LOW` a frame can recentre without a step, and a stale texture draws the whole fog 8 cells (640 units) off for that frame. Resolution divisor `FogMode.divisor`: `HIGH` 1, `DEFAULT` 2, `LOW` 4; `OFF` draws the fog that is left like `DEFAULT`. Above 1 the quad is drawn with the same projection into a framebuffer of the GL viewport size / divisor (`beginTarget`), then one textured quad stretches it back over the drawn area with linear filtering (`endTarget`, texture coordinates from the viewport's visible rectangle). The fog has no detail under about 40 units, so a quarter resolution draw was within 2/255 per pixel of full resolution at zoom 0.5 to 3 in a headless render. The texture packs density / 1.3, the advected detail offset and the flow speed. The shader reads it through a cubic B-spline (`g`, four linear taps), so the edges of trails and holes show no cell steps at close zoom; the texture filter must stay linear. Alpha rises over a long soft ramp (`cover / 0.85`, smoothstep, power 1.1). Tendrils are the mask's arms only; the shader draws no wisps. Colours: `FogRenderer.coloursFor` takes the average colour of the system's first `nebula` terrain texture (`BaseTiledTerrain.getParams()` cat and key, `SpriteAPI.getAverageColor`), scaled to a dark, body and light shade; `FogRenderer.GREY` without a nebula. Cached per plugin while loaded.
- Sunlight: `readSuns` passes up to `FogRenderer.MAX_SUNS` 3 stars per frame (place, radius, light reach in bank local coordinates) and their shades. The shader takes the strongest star per pixel: full at the surface, gone `SUN_LIGHT_DIAMETERS` 1.5 star diameters out (a star under `STAR_UNIT_MIN` 350 radius counts as 350) (edge wobbled by the fog noise); there the fog's three shades blend to the star's, the colour is pulled 60% to the star's light shade and alpha rises by up to 60%. `FogRenderer.sunShades`: the star spec's icon colour pulled `SUN_WHITE` 35% to white (the icon colours are fully saturated, the corona colours of orange and yellow stars are nearly the same), through the same `shadesOf` as the nebula colour. No light from nebula centres, black holes and icons darker than `SUN_MIN_BRIGHTNESS` 0.5. The plain fallback mesh draws no sunlight.
- Clear rings are drawn per pixel, not from the grid: a ring round a small star or a moon is only 2 to 4 cells wide, too fine for the grid. The grid holds `Shape.gridMask` (no rings); `FogRenderer.putRings` passes the rings of `Shape.keep` that touch the drawn area (up to `MAX_RINGS` 32, the widest win) and `keep()` in the fragment shader multiplies the density by them, the same math as `Shape.keepAt`. Star rings rise by smoothstep; plain rings (planets, moons, stations, jump points, markets) rise linearly from inner to outer, and in the shader only their distance is bent by up to 17% with the fog detail noise (`n3`), so the edge is soft and not a true circle. The lit rim comes from the grid's gradient alone, so a clear ring draws no bright outline. `moveKeep` moves each ring to its body every frame while the grid is live (`keepBodies`, same order as `Shape.keep`); the rings themselves are rebuilt every `KEEP_REFRESH_DAYS` 0.1 days. The plain fallback mesh multiplies `keepAt` per vertex. Holes cut by movers (planet wakes, trails) stay in the grid.
- The second texture packs `heat`, `ion`, `flash`, `wake` per cell (`FogGrid.packEffects`). It is uploaded and read (`uFxOn`, four more taps through the same B-spline) only while `FogGrid.hasEffects()`, so quiet fog costs no more than before.
- `renderOnMap` draws the rest mask on a 40 x 40 grid out to 1.4 radius in the light shade, alpha 0.35 x mask x strength, so the map shows the real outline.

### Shot effects

Look approved on the mockup page (project files `ie/FOG_SHOT_EFFECTS_MOCKUP.html`, picks of 2026-11-28): railgun look E (hair thin trail, heated wake, hot line), blast heat in reds only, ion storm look B (Flicker), missiles by warhead, shock wave to 0.6 explosion radii. Effects only tint fog; nothing is drawn in clear space. Look changes go through that page. Times below are real seconds of flight.

- Effect fields (`FogGrid.heat`, `ion`, `flash`, `wake`, 0..1 per cell): raised by `disc` (full inside half the radius, soft to 1.3 radii), `splat` (falls off squared) and `line` (along a segment, linear to the radius), advected with the fog and fading with `HEAT_TAU` 3.4 s, `ION_TAU` 2.6 s, `FLASH_TAU` 0.11 s, `WAKE_TAU` 2.25 s (flashes do not move). Below `EFFECT_FLOOR` (half a byte step) they are neither stepped nor drawn. Recentring shifts them with the grid.
- Mortar and railgun hits: a `VariableExplosionEntityPlugin` with `artilleryType` set (`getVariableParams()`) blasts as above and adds `shockWave`: a `Ring` out to `WAVE` 1.1 x the crater radius, so 0.6 explosion radii, over 0.6 s (the ring Interdiction Pulse uses: clears behind it, piles fog on its rim) and heat 1 out to `WAVE_HEAT` 1.25 crater radii. Mortar 300: ring to 180, heat to 205. Railgun hit 250: ring to 150, heat to 170.
- Railgun lane (`lanceOnce`, once when `isFired()` turns true, not window checked): from `BEAM_START` 40 past `originLocation` to the beam end. Up to `FogRenderer.MAX_LANES` 8 (the oldest is dropped), dropped after `LANE_LIFE` 11 s; only the segment and its age reach the shader, which holds the trail numbers. Trail: no fog inside 3 units, full fog from 16 x (1 + 1.5 grow), `grow = 1 - exp(-age / 1.6)`. The fog carries it off line by `((n2 - 0.5) x 420 + (n3 - 0.5) x 110) x grow` and it tears instead of closing evenly: depth `(1 - smoothstep(0.15, 1, age / 1.8 + brk)) x mix(1, 0.4, grow)`, `brk = ((n3 - 0.5) x 3.4 + (n2 - 0.5) x 1.2) x grow`. Hot line along the drifting trail: 1.25 for 0.12 s, then 1.1, decaying with tau 2.25 s, Gaussian width 34. Wake: `FogGrid.line` puts `wake` 1 out to `LANE_WAKE_RADIUS` 150 once at fire; it drifts with the fog. The 80 unit grid cannot hold the trail, so the grid only gets a sideways push (`FogGrid.lance` with `cut` false, `LANE_STIR_WIDTH` 104, `LANE_STIR_PUSH` about 97). With the plain mesh (`FogRenderer.isPlain()`) the grid cut of `LANCE_INNER` 50 / `LANCE_OUTER` 130 is used instead.
- Charge trails: carriers `CARRIER_CHARGE` 0.55, submunitions `SUBMUNITION_CHARGE` 0.4, consumable missiles `MISSILE_CHARGE` 0.5, splatted every frame over `CHARGE_RADIUS` 120.
- Consumable missiles (`missileShot`) hit once when `BaseMissileEntityPlugin.finishing` turns true (`onExplosion` has run, the entity holds still while it fades). By warhead: explosive = `shockWave` for `EXPLOSION_SIZE` 250, ring to 150 (its own explosion cuts the crater); concussive = `FogGrid.shove` to `SHOVE_RANGE` 500 with `CONCUSSIVE_PUSH` 420 (outward, thinned in the middle, no hole); smoke = trail only; intercept = a `Ring` to `INTERDICT_RANGE` 300 and an ion storm of 300 for `INTERDICT_SECONDS` 6. Other `BaseMissileEntityPlugin` subclasses leave the trail only.
- Ion storms (`stormStep`): the charge is held at `STORM_CHARGE` 0.95 out to the storm radius every frame while it lasts, and a flash of 170..320 units (0.55..1) drops at a random point inside every 0.06..0.25 x (220 / radius + 0.35) s. Each `ECMExplosion` slow field in the window is a storm of `FIELD_STORM` 1.25 x its `rad` (250 at the default 200) until `finishing`; then the charge fades by itself in about 5 s. Intercept storms live in the transient `storms` list. Flashes use `Misc.random`.
- Shader (`indevo_fogFragment.shader`, after the sunlight): trail cut and hot line per pixel. Blast heat (`heat`, x 0.8..1.2 by noise) tints the fog in reds only, dark red to bright red. Railgun heat `max(wake x 1.2 x 0.8..1.2, hot line)` tints it through `heatCol` (white hot, yellow, orange, red, dark red); thin fog runs cooler. The charge barely tints the fog (0.12, broken up by one extra noise read at about 110 units); a flash lights the fog round it blue to purple, multiplied by the fog's brightness, with a pale blue core. All of it is colour on fog (`c * a`), no additive light, so cleared space and clear patches round fleets stay dark.
- Low Fidelity: movers, blasts, discs and splats feed every frame, the effect fields step with the solver (at most 30 Hz). The tint is soft, the trail draws softer at quarter resolution.

### Cache

- `spawnCache` (`FogWeatherManager.java:337`): banks only, `BANK_CACHE_CHANCE` 15%. One of `supply_cache_small`, `equipment_cache_small`, `weapons_cache_small` through `BaseThemeGenerator.addSalvageEntity` (faction neutral, vanilla salvage and discovery). Placed at the thickest full mask of up to 30 tries, 0.1..0.35 radius from the centre, on a 300 day orbit round the fog entity so it moves with the bank.
- `hideCache` (`FogTerrainPlugin.java:309`), each frame in the player's location and once at spawn: `1 - 0.7 * density` as a mult on the cache's `getDetectedRangeMod()`. Density is `getDensityAt`; during the roll in the rest mask is used without the strength, so the cache never sits in the open first. During the fade the cut shrinks with the fog.
- Spot range of a small cache is `(sensor strength + profile 1 + 1200) x cache mult x own sensor mult` (`BaseCampaignEntity.getBaseSensorRangeToDetect`, `getMaxSensorRangeToDetect`, knowledge skill `obf/campaign/BaseCampaignEntity.java:1089`; detection range 1200 from `salvage_entity_gen_data.csv`). Estimate for a fleet sensor strength of 450..1050: 1650..2250 in the open, 500..675 from outside thick fog, 50..70 from inside it, about 1400..1600 with Active Sensor Burst (3000 flat, own cut skipped).
- When the fog is over the plugin calls `Misc.fadeAndExpire` on a cache that is still there. A looted cache expires the vanilla way and the plugin drops its reference.

### Lurkers

- Built with `FleetFactoryV3`, pirates 70% else Luddic Path, 30..70 combat points, fleet type `patrolMedium`, transponder off, `$IndEvo_fogLurker`. The post is a random point in the fog (best full mask of 30 tries, `getFullMaskAt`, 0.2..0.7 radius out), kept as an offset from the fog centre so it moves with a bank.
- `FogLurkerAssignmentAI.advance` (`FogLurkerAssignmentAI.java:53`): `HOLD` at the post (re-issued when the post moved more than 600 units). A trail is noticed (`noticesTrail`, `:105`) only for the player fleet, within 1500 units, not `Misc.isSlowMoving`, inside fog (density above 0.05), and only if `fleet.isHostileTo(player)`. Then `INTERCEPT` the player. The lurker cannot see the player in fog, and the vanilla tactical module sends a fleet with an unseen target to `$ai_lastSeenTargetLoc`, or to a random point 3000 units away when that is unset (knowledge skill `obf/campaign/ai/TacticalModule.java:573`), so while the trail is noticed the script writes the player's position to `FleetAIFlags.LAST_SEEN_TARGET_LOC` and clears `DAYS_TARGET_UNSEEN` each frame. When the trail stops the vanilla search pattern runs round the last point; 2 days after the last sighting the lurker returns to the post. When the fog is gone it goes to the nearest jump point (else the system centre) with `GO_TO_LOCATION_AND_DESPAWN`.
- `HOLD` never engages, so a player going slow or dark past a lurker is safe; the vanilla game never reads `TerrainAIFlags`, so this script is the only fog behaviour NPCs have.

## Contracts

- `FogGrid` and `FogNoise` import nothing from the game. Keep it that way: the headless check compiles them alone.
- The rest mask is a pure function of local position, seed and time, so the shape follows an orbiting bank and is the same after a load. The grid is never saved.
- Strength, the end of the fog and the mask time come only from the saved start timestamp and the campaign clock.
- Effects run for fleets within `getRenderRange()` (`:357`) of the fog centre: `BaseTerrain.advance` skips fleets farther out (knowledge skill `api/impl/campaign/terrain/BaseTerrain.java:89`), and `CampaignTerrain.render` only calls `render` when the centre is within it of the viewport (`obf/campaign/CampaignTerrain.java:88`).
- Spot range is `(observer sensor strength + target profile) x target detected range mods x observer sensor range mods` (knowledge skill `obf/campaign/BaseCampaignEntity.java:1089`), so the cut on both sides multiplies. `isFogContact` reads the applied stat mods by `DETECTED_MOD_ID` and `SENSOR_MOD_ID`; `applyEffect` must keep writing them under those ids.
- `ShaderLib.loadShader` returns 0 when the shader fails (GraphicsLib 1.9.0). `FogRenderer` then logs a warning once and draws the plain mesh.
- The reduced resolution draw uses core framebuffers (`OpenGL30`) or `EXT_framebuffer_object`, the same order GraphicsLib's `ShaderLib.init` uses apart from ARB. Without either, or when the buffer is incomplete, it logs a warning once and draws at full resolution. It saves and restores the bound framebuffer (`GL_FRAMEBUFFER_BINDING`, the game or GraphicsLib may draw into its own) and the viewport, clear colour, blend, enable and scissor state (`glPushAttrib`).
- Fog shader uniforms and the texture layouts are coupled between `FogRenderer.renderShader`, `FogGrid.pack`, `FogGrid.packEffects`, `FogTerrainPlugin.advanceLanes` (`FogRenderer.LANE_STRIDE`) and `indevo_fogFragment.shader`. The effect texture is on texture unit 1; `renderShader` sets unit 0 active again before the framebuffer stretch and on return. `keep()` in the shader and `FogGrid.Shape.keepAt` must stay the same math, apart from the shader's edge wobble: effects read `keepAt`, the player sees `keep()`.
- Couplings: `MeteorSwarmManager.METEOR_TAG` ([Meteor](METEOR.md)); the artillery projectile plugins, `RailgunProjectileEntityPlugin.isFired()`, `originLocation` and `BEAM_START`, `VariableExplosionEntityPlugin.getVariableParams().artilleryType`, `ECMExplosion.finishing` and `rad` ([Artillery](../industries/ARTILLERY.md)); `BaseMissileEntityPlugin.finishing` and the warhead constants of the explosive, concussive and intercept missiles ([Consumables](../items/CONSUMABLES.md)); `MiscIE.getRandomInRange` ([Utils](../core/UTILS.md)); `Setting.FOG_WEATHER` through `FogMode` ([Settings](../core/SETTINGS.md)).
- `AchievementCampaignListener` reads `MEM_LURKER` on battle fleets ([ACHIEVEMENTS](../core/ACHIEVEMENTS.md)).
