# Pets

The Animal Center industry (`IndEvo_PetStore`), the pet submarket, pet items, pet hullmods, pet life cycle and the Pet Management ability. Read it before changing pets, pet food, pet storage or `data/campaign/pets.csv`. It does not cover the abandoned pet center derelict ([Stations](../exploration/STATIONS.md)), how submarkets are added and removed ([Submarkets](../core/SUBMARKETS.md)) or the ghost ship hullmods that reuse `PetHullmod` ([Distress](../exploration/DISTRESS.md)).

Checked against Starsector 0.98a-RC8 and IE `cbdc2e1`. Recheck engine facts after a game update.

## Overview

A pet is a `Pet` object bound to one fleet member (perma hullmod plus variant tag) or stored in an Animal Center. Pets age, eat food commodities from fleet cargo (or from the storage their ship sits in) once a month, and die of old age, starvation, combat loss, sale or euthanasia. The pet's hullmod effect grows with time served. The Animal Center sells pets as `IndEvo_PetBox` special items, stores pets and opens the Pets panel. The `pet_management` ability opens the same panel in flight, without storage and Rehome. Pets in the fleet post short lines about what they are up to, in flight and when docking.

## Registration and lifecycle

- Industry, submarket, item and ability plugins from CSV plugin columns.
- `PetManagementAbilityPlugin.applySetting()` (`abilities/pets/PetManagementAbilityPlugin.java:19-43`): with `IndEvo_pets` on, adds `pet_management` (`Ids.ABILITY_PET_MANAGEMENT`) to the player fleet and character, clears `no_sell` on `fairy` once research `Ids.PROJ_NAVI` is redeemed ([Research](../dialogue/RESEARCH.md)) and loads pet textures; a texture that fails to load throws `IllegalStateException`. With the setting off, removes the ability. `PlayerSettings.applyCampaignSettings()` runs it from `ModPlugin.onGameLoad()` (`utils/ModPlugin.java:171`) and after a Save in the LunaLib menu in the campaign ([Settings](../core/SETTINGS.md#reading-a-setting)). On new game with pets on, `onGameLoad` also adds Lord Foog (`:173`).
- `IndEvo_PetDockingLine` is found through the `ruleCommandPackages` entry `indevo.industries.petshop.rules` ([Rule commands](../dialogue/RULE_COMMANDS.md)).
- `PetCenterOptionProvider.register()` from `setListenersIfNeeded()` (`ModPlugin.java:466`).
- `PetStatusManager.getInstance()` (`PetStatusManager.java:75`) creates the manager on first use: persistent listener, sector script, player fleet event listener. Found again later by `hasListenerOfClass`.
- `PetShop.apply()` adds itself as a persistent listener if missing (`PetShop.java:174`). Storage fee: `getTotalStorageCost()` sums `getStorageCost()` over live pets (`STORAGE_FEE_FRACT` 10% of the species `value` a month, at least `MIN_STORAGE_FEE` 100, x `NPC_STORAGE_FEE_MULT` 1.5 on NPC markets, then x `petStorageCostMult`; the species value, never the pet's own, so the fee shows no unread traits). Player markets pay it as Animal Center upkeep (`IndEvo_petStorage`, `:163`), so the hazard and AI core upkeep mults apply; the tooltip total includes them. NPC markets bill it in `reportEconomyTick()` on the "Pet storage" report line ([BillingHelper](../core/UTILS.md#billinghelper)). `advance()` keeps the pet submarket through `IndustrySubmarkets.ensure` while pets are enabled and removes it while they are off (`:146-147`); `notifyBeingRemoved()` releases it (`:153`).
- Animal Centers are placed at game start at `jangala`, `gilead`, `fikenhild` ([Utils](../core/UTILS.md)).

## Flow

### Getting a pet

`PetSubMarket` restock -> `IndEvo_PetBox` (Cryochamber) in cargo -> right-click in fleet cargo tab (`PetItemPlugin.performRightClickAction(RightClickActionHelper)`) -> `PetPlaceDialog.chamber(data, helper).show` opens the popup over the cargo screen through `UiHost.over` (`showInteractionDialogFromCargo`).

- Popup: "Open Cryochamber", the portrait, a name field with a suggested name (`PetDataRepo.suggestName`), Species and Life span ("About 5 cycles", days under a cycle, from `maxlife`); "To a ship without a pet" (`PetStatusManager.getFreeShips`, the first picked) and "To the menagerie" (picked when no ship is free). No storage row: the cargo screen opens anywhere.
- Confirm: `new Pet` with the typed name (an empty field keeps the suggested one, at most `NAME_MAX_CHARS`) -> `PetStatusManager.register` -> `Pet.place(ship or null, null)` -> one box removed through `helper.removeFromClickedStackFirst(1)`. Cancel: nothing changes, the box stays. Both return to the cargo screen.

- Restock: weighted picker over pets without `no_sell`, weight = rarity (x1.3 with an alpha core when rarity < 0.5). Picks = market size; amount per pick = floor(rarity x 3), at least 1.
- Tariff 0.2, 0 with a beta core. Selling to the submarket is illegal.
- Drops (`resolveDropParams`): tier rare rarity <= 0.4, uncommon 0.4 to 0.8, common >= 0.8; `no_drop` pets skipped. Price = pet `value`.

### Assigned pet

- `place(member, storage)` is the only method that moves a pet: it takes the pet off its ship and out of storage, then puts it on the member or into the Animal Center storage (both null: on no ship and in no storage). On a ship it adds `PetData.hullmod` as a perma mod and the `PET_DATA_ID_` tag and switches to a custom variant unless called from refit.
- Hearts (`Pet.getHearts()`, 0 to 5) are read from `bondDays` against `HEART_DAYS` 14, 60, 180, 300, 480 and never stored. `Pet.advance()` adds a day of `bondDays` for every day aboard (`isAboard()`: on a ship of a live fleet, the player's or a detachment's), a quarter on Supplies (`SUPPLIES_HEART_RATE`, last meal was Supplies), none while starving or Unsettled, and stops at 180, the third heart (`TIME_HEARTS`). Hearts 4 and 5 come only from answered wants and shared events, and only for a favourite ([Favourites and wants](#favourites-and-wants)), and from `indevo_pet hearts`. A move keeps hearts.
- Mascot effect (`Pet.getMascotMult()`): 0 under 1 heart (`MASCOT_HEARTS`) or while Unsettled, else 1, x2 for the hamster pace (`HAMSTER_MULT`), x1.3 with a nest (`NEST_MULT`). `PetHullmod` applies CR loss -5% x that. `getEffectFract()` returns the same for copies of `YourMod_PetName_PetHullmod`. Pet hullmods look up their pet through `PetStatusManager.getPet(variant)`.
- Nest (`Pet.hasNest()`): aboard with at least 4 hearts (`NEST_HEARTS`) and `nestRebuildDays` at 0. `nestRebuildDays` counts down only aboard.
- A pet that arrives on a ship other than `homeShipId`, the last ship it lived on, is Unsettled for `UNSETTLED_DAYS` (30), `NEST_UNSETTLED_DAYS` (90) when it had a nest, which also sets `nestRebuildDays` to `NEST_REBUILD_DAYS` (120): Move... and Assign... in the Pets panel, a hand over, an escape pod to another ship. Its first ship (from a Cryochamber) and a return to the same ship after storage are no move. While Unsettled, `PetHullmod` applies no effect and its tooltip shows the days left.
- `reportPlayerEngagement` sets every pet on a deployed or retreated player ship Shell shocked for `SHELL_SHOCKED_DAYS` (7).
- Mood (`Pet.getMood()`, first match wins): Hungry (starving, or not stored and `PetStatusManager.hasFood` false: no food for its next meal), Unsettled, Shell shocked, Sulking (`isSulking()`: `SULK_LAPSES`, 3, wants lapsed in a row), Unhappy with its food (last meal was Supplies), Happy (petted or a want answered in the last `HAPPY_DAYS`, 3), Sleepy (age over 80% of `lifetime`), Content. Display only, shown in the hullmod tooltip and on the card. Sulking ends with the next answered want or an unstar.

### Favourites and wants

- Favourite (`Pet.favourite`, the star on the card): at most `MAX_FAVOURITES` (3) living pets (`PetStatusManager.canFavourite`). `register()` stars a new pet when there is no favourite. Unstarring ends its want (cooldown and fleet gap, no lapse) and clears its lapses.
- `Pet.addBond(days)` adds bond days up to the third heart, up to the fifth for a favourite. Shared events add `EVENT_BOND_DAYS` (3) to every pet they include: a jump of the player fleet to the pets aboard its ships (`reportFleetJumped`), a fight to the pets on deployed or retreated ships (`reportPlayerEngagement`).
- Want timer (`PetStatusManager.advanceWants`, every frame of the sector script): one want open in the whole fleet. When none is open and `wantGapDays` is 0, a random favourite with the player (`Pet.isWithPlayer()`: on a ship of the player fleet or in the menagerie) whose `wantCooldownDays` is 0 opens a random want: Attention, Play, Shore leave, Come along, and Treat when it has a favourite food (`PetData.getFavouriteFood()`, the first food that is not Supplies). Comfort opens after a fight instead (`reportPlayerEngagement`), under the same rules; in a battle the member sits in the battle's combined fleet, so the handler takes "with the player" from `BattleAPI.getSourceFleet(member)`. `openWant` says the `want_<id>` line.
- A want stays open `WANT_DAYS` (30) while the pet is with the player; at 0 it lapses (`lapsedWants` +1). A pet that leaves the player fleet (stored, out with a detachment) drops its want, no lapse. Every end sets the pet's cooldown to `WANT_COOLDOWN_DAYS` (60) and the fleet gap to `WANT_GAP_DAYS` (5).
- Answers (`Pet.answerWant()`): `WANT_BOND_DAYS` (30) bond days, Happy, lapses back to 0. Attention and Comfort: petting (`Pet.pet()`). Play: the Play button on the card (`Pet.play()`). Treat: the Treat button takes 1 of the favourite food from the cargo it eats from (`PetStatusManager.treat`). Shore leave: opening a real market (`reportPlayerOpenedMarket`). Come along: loot shown (`reportAboutToShowLootToPlayer`), a planet surveyed (`reportPlayerSurveyedPlanet`) or an entity discovered (`reportEntityDiscovered`). The last three answer every open want of that kind with the player.
- A favourite weighs x3 as a line speaker (`FAVOURITE_LINE_WEIGHT`), on top of the flagship weight ([Lines](#lines)). The card's Play and Treat buttons show a `play` or `treat` line in the tagline.

### Talents

- Sheet (`Pet.sheet`, `PetSheet`): `new Pet` rolls it with `PetGenetics.rollSheet`: a random disposition of the five officer personalities, and 1 or 2 talents (`TWO_TALENTS_CHANCE`, 0.5) at grade 1, picked by `weight` x the species lean. The lean is the `pets.csv` `talents` column, `name:weight` space separated (`scavenger:3`, the id without `IndEvo_pt_`); a talent not listed weighs 1. A talent worth nothing at grade 1 (Pathfinder, Brood deck, Tinkerer: +1 per 4 or 5 grades) is never rolled; they come from raising. A rare species (`PetData.isRare()`, [Rare] and [Epic]) brings one signature talent besides at `SIGNATURE_CHANCE` (0.2), by the same weights.
- `pet_traits.csv`: `kind` `stat` (on its own ship), `fleet` (once per fleet) or `signature` (on its own ship, rolled only for rare species). `stat` is a case of `PetHullmod.applyStat`. Value (`Trait.value`): `perLevel` per full step of `per` grades (blank 1), x `Pet.getTalentMult()`, then at most `cap`. `mode`: `percent` (+N%), `reduce` (-N%, applied as a multiplier 1 - N/100, cap 90), `flat` (+N), `count` (+N rounded to a whole number). `effect` is the card label, `desc` the tooltip with `%s` for the value of one step, `flavour` a tooltip line with `%s` for the pet name.
- Stat keys that are not a plain percent: `max_cr` and `recovery` add points (value x 0.01, as vanilla skills do); `recovery` (Lucky charm) also cuts `DMOD_ACQUIRE_PROB_MOD` by the same value, at most `PetHullmod.DMOD_CAP` (90). `weapon_range` is ballistic and energy, `rate_of_fire` all three weapon kinds, `maneuverability` acceleration, deceleration, turn acceleration and turn rate.
- Signature stat keys: `time_mult` (Time skip) `getTimeMult()`, as Temporal Shell but always on; `deployment` (Small target) `Stats.DEPLOYMENT_POINTS_MOD`, read by `FleetMember` for the deployment points cost (`obf/campaign/fleet/FleetMember.java:941`); `hull_regen` (Mender) a flat `PetHullmod.MENDER` mod that `advanceInCombat` reads each frame, percent of max hull a second while the ship is alive; `system` (Overcharge) `getSystemRegenBonus()` percent and `getSystemUsesBonus()` +1 per `PetHullmod.OVERCHARGE_STEP` (30) of the value, as Systems Expertise; `min_crew` (Skeleton crew) `getMinCrewMod()` mult, the one reduction that goes to 100% (`PetChecks.FULL_REDUCE`), as Automated; `explosion` (Spite) `Stats.EXPLOSION_RADIUS_MULT` and `EXPLOSION_DAMAGE_MULT`, read by `DamagingExplosionSpec.explosionSpecForShip` (radius is collision radius + min(200, collision radius) x mult); `fighter_bays` (Brood deck) `getNumFighterBays()` flat, as Converted Hangar; `smods` (Tinkerer) `Stats.MAX_PERMANENT_HULLMODS_MOD` flat, read from the member stats by `Misc.getMaxPermanentMods`, as Best of the Best; `captain` (Captain) no stat, see below.
- Fleet talents are ship stats the game sums over the fleet: Pathfinder `Stats.FLEET_BURN_BONUS` (`FleetData` sync, as Drive Field Stabilizer), Marine mascot `Stats.FLEET_GROUND_SUPPORT` (`Misc.getFleetwideTotalMod`, as Ground Support Package), Scavenger `Stats.SALVAGE_VALUE_MULT_MOD` (as a Salvage Gantry: combined over the fleet with diminishing returns, `RepairGantry.getAdjustedGantryModifier`, ships under 10% CR left out; rare drops use the fleet stat `SALVAGE_VALUE_MULT_FLEET_INCLUDES_RARE` in `SalvageEntity.performSalvage`, so Scavenger does not reach them). Only the ship of the lead pet applies one: `PetStatusManager.getLead(fleet, id)`, the highest value on a ship that is not mothballed, then more hearts, then the lower pet id.
- Scavenger find (`PetStatusManager.scavenge`, from `reportAboutToShowLootToPlayer`): loot of a salvaged entity (target tagged `Tags.SALVAGEABLE`; battle loot has a fleet as target) with a lead Scavenger in the player fleet gets a find at `SCAVENGER_FIND_CHANCE` (0.25), seeded by `Misc.getSalvageSeed(target)` so a reload finds the same. The find is `SalvageEntity.generateSalvage` with a `Drops.BASIC` value drop of `SCAVENGER_FIND_VALUE` (1000) credits x (1 + value / 100) and one roll on `rare_tech_low` (colony items, 90% nothing), added to the loot. The loot screen hides the dialog text (`InteractionDialog.showCoreInternal`, `obf/ui/newui/N.java:958`) and vanilla salvage closes the dialog after it, so `reportFind` posts the `scavenger` line with the found items as a `MessageIntel`. A debris field rolls again on each pass, with the new seed vanilla sets.
- Active (`Pet.getActiveTalents()`): talents best grade first, ties in arrival order (`PetGenetics.talents`; an id no loaded mod has is left out), one per heart, all at 5 hearts (`activeCount`). Talent mult (`getTalentMult()`): 0 while Unsettled, x2 for the hamster, x1.3 with a nest. A pet on no ship has no effect.
- Reading (`PetSheet.known`): a talent is read when its heart switches it on, and every talent at `READ_HEARTS` (3). `Pet.effectsChanged()` posts "<name> the <species> shows a talent: <talent>." for each (held while the Pets panel is open). `indevo_pet trait` reads the trait it sets.
- Ship stats change only on a fleet sync. `Pet.syncShip()`, from `Pet.effectsChanged()` (hearts or the talent mult changed, from `advance`, `addBond` and `setBondDays`), `place()` (the old and the new ship) and `setTrait()`, calls `FleetDataAPI.setSyncNeeded()`; the next `CampaignFleet.advance` runs `syncIfNeeded` (`obf/campaign/fleet/CampaignFleet.java:661`), which calls `updateStats` on every member (`obf/campaign/fleet/FleetData.java:651-700`), so the lead pets are picked again too. For a ship of a fleet it also runs `PetHullmod.updateShip(member, pet)` at once: `updateStats`, then, when the room its Tinkerer gives fell since the last sync, the cheapest s-mods over the limit go, at most as many as the room fell, as vanilla does on a skill loss (`SkillsChangeRemoveSmodsEffect`). The room is the flat `MAX_PERMANENT_HULLMODS_MOD` bonus under the pet's hullmod id; the old value is kept in `Pet.sModRoom` (0 after `leave`), since the member stats can already be rebuilt when `updateShip` runs. A fighter in a bay past the new bay count goes to the fleet cargo; `PetStatusManager.reportRoomLost` names what went. A ship in market storage (no fleet) or no longer in its fleet (lost) is left as it is.
- Captain: `Pet.updateCaptain()` (`Pet.java:152`), from `syncShip`, gives the pet a person while the talent is on and the pet is aboard: `PetHullmod.createCaptain` (the pet name, its icon as portrait, its disposition as personality, tag `Ids.TAG_PET_CAPTAIN`) with `round(value)` combat skills from `fitCaptain`: the vanilla officer pool (the personnel officer filter of `OfficerManagerEvent`), shuffled by the pet id so a new grade adds one skill, elite past the pool size, level = skills. It is saved in `Pet.captain` and takes the seat when it is free (no captain or a default one). The portrait keeps it from counting as default (`Person.isDefault()` compares the portrait with `misc/default_portrait`, `obf/rpg/Person.java:115`). It is not in the officer list, so it draws no pay (`Misc.getCombatPay`, the officer payroll), gains no XP and never dies as an officer (`FleetEncounterContext.java:505` skips captains without `OfficerData`). An officer, an AI core or the player in the seat keeps it; a right-click unassign or a new officer deletes the person from the seat. No API callback tells of that, so `Pet.advance` checks the seat each frame while `Pet.captain` is set (`Pet.java:312`) and sits back down when it is free. The talent off (Unsettled, fewer hearts) or `leave()` takes it off the ship and clears `Pet.captain`; a rename updates its first name (`Pet.rename`).

### Titles

- `PetTitle`, earned once each through `Pet.earn` (in `Pet.titles` as the constant name, in the order earned; a pet from a save before titles gets an empty list in `getTitleIds()`, an unknown name is left out by `getTitles()`). `PetStatusManager.reportTitle` posts "<name> the <species> earned a title: <title>." (held while the Pets panel is open).
- Veteran: `VETERAN_FIGHTS` (10) fights, counted in `Pet.fights` by `reportPlayerEngagement` for a pet whose ship deployed. Navigator: `NAVIGATOR_JUMPS` (50) jumps of the player fleet with the pet aboard (`Pet.jumps`, `reportFleetJumped`). Old Timer: age past the species `maxLife` (`Pet.advance`). Survivor: a pet that got out by escape pod (`escapeOrDie`). Beloved: five hearts (`Pet.effectsChanged`). Pampered: `PAMPERED_WANTS` (10) answered wants (`Pet.answeredWants`, `answerWant`). Treasure Hunter: `TREASURE_HUNTER_FINDS` (5) Scavenger finds (`Pet.finds`, `scavenge`). Admiral: `ADMIRAL_DAYS` (365) days on the flagship of the player fleet (`Pet.flagshipDays`, `Pet.advance`). Globetrotter: `GLOBETROTTER_MARKETS` (25) different real markets opened with the pet along (`Pet.markets`, `reportPlayerOpenedMarket`; the set is cleared once earned, an older save gets one on first use). Stargazer: `STARGAZER_DAYS` (120) days with the player fleet in hyperspace (`Pet.hyperspaceDays`, `Pet.advance`).
- Shown on the card as the Titles row under the hearts bar ("None" in grey; a list longer than the row ends in "..."; the tooltip names each title and its reason) and in the pet hover as a Titles row when there is one. Champion is kept for shows (not built).

### Daily and monthly

- `Pet.advance()` ages the pet (not in storage under a beta core, `Pet.java:280`), counts down the Unsettled and Shell shocked timers and feeds it every 31 days. Feeding takes `foodPerMonth` of the first listed food commodity the cargo holds enough of, Supplies only when no other listed food is there (`lastMealSupplies` then set), from the fleet cargo; for a ship in a submarket, from the storage cargo of that market (`PetStatusManager.getFoodCargo`, `PetStatusManager.java:381`); no food -> starving -> `STARVED` death.
- Age past `lifetime` -> `NATURAL` death, message from `natural_death` or the hamster CSV.
- `PetStatusManager`: month end food report; month end alive check kills a pet (`UNKNOWN`) whose ship has no fleet data or sits in a dead fleet, but not one in storage, whose fleet data has no fleet (`PetStatusManager.java:408`); battle losses below.

### Battles

- `PetStatusManager.reportBattleOccurred(primaryWinner, battle)`, the global campaign listener report, walks `battle.getSnapshotBothSides()` for the player fleet and detachment fleets (`FleetUtils.isDetachmentFleet`); the fleet event report is empty. The global report also comes for AI battles of detachments, once per autoresolve round (`api/campaign/CampaignEventListener.java:51-56`; both reports come from `CampaignEngine.reportBattleOccurred`, `obf/campaign/CampaignEngine.java:2123-2141`).
- A living pet on a ship in `Misc.getSnapshotMembersLost(fleet)` rolls an escape pod at `POD_CHANCE_PER_HEART` (10%) per heart. Survivor: Shell shocked, on a free ship of the detachment (`getFreeShip(fleet, lost)`), else in the menagerie, with a message. Failed roll: `COMBAT` death. A later autoresolve round reports the same snapshot; the dead pet and the survivor are no longer on a lost ship, so nothing happens twice.

### Hand over and menagerie

- A ship sold, stored (`reportPlayerMarketTransaction`) or scuttled (`PetScuttlePatch`) hands its pet to a free ship of the player fleet through `handOver`: `getFreeShip` takes the flagship if it has no pet, else the largest hull size, then the highest deployment cost, never a fighter wing or a ship with a pet or a ship that is leaving. The pet is placed there (a move, so Unsettled) and a message names both ships.
- No free ship: a sold ship's pet dies (`SOLD`), a stored ship's pet stays aboard with the forgotten pet warning, a scuttled ship's pet dies at once (`UNKNOWN`), as at month end before.
- Menagerie: a living pet with no ship and no storage (`Pet.isInMenagerie()`). It ages and eats from the player fleet cargo, has no effect, and shows in the Pets panel under Menagerie, where it can be assigned or stored. An escape pod survivor from the player fleet lands there, and `indevo_pet give` without a free ship puts a pet there.
- `reportPetDied()` ignores a pet that is already dead, removes the hullmod and tag, posts a message, keeps `DeathData`.

### Lines

- `pet_lines.csv`: one row per line. `event` is `ambient`, `docking`, `status`, `petting`, `petting_starving`, `name`, `play`, `treat`, `scavenger` (the Scavenger find), `jump`, `fight` or a want (`want_attention`, `want_play`, `want_treat`, `want_comfort`, `want_shore_leave`, `want_come_along`). `match` holds pet ids, body tags (`b_`) or class tags (`c_`), space separated, or `any`. `mood` (`PetMood.id()`), `disposition` (officer personality id) and `context` (`docked`, `storage`, `old`, `hyperspace`) are space separated sets; blank or `any` matches all. `text` has one `%s` for the pet name, shown highlighted. A `name` row holds a suggested name as its text, with no `%s`, mood, disposition or context; `suggestName` picks one the same way, the species name when none fits.
- `pets.csv` `body`: `b_fourleg`, `b_legs`, `b_wings`, `b_water`, `b_float`, `b_blob`, `b_machine`, `b_jar`, `b_coil`, `b_human`. A row without it (another mod's pet) matches only class and `any` lines.
- `PetDataRepo.pickLine`: every row of the event whose mood, disposition and context fit goes into one weighted pick. Weight by the best `match` token that fits: pet id 4, body 2, class 1.5, `any` 1. Class and `any` lines describe no body.
- `PetStatusManager.getLine(pet, event, docked)` builds the context: `storage` for a stored pet, else `docked` when docked; `old` past 80% of `lifetime` (`Pet.isOld()`); `hyperspace` while the player fleet is in hyperspace (lines about a jump). `say(pet, event, dialog)` adds the line to the dialog text, or posts a `MessageIntel` with the pet's icon when there is no dialog. Docked means the dialog target has a real market.
- Cadence (`advanceLines`, saved `daysSinceLine`, `lineRoll`): in flight, an ambient line comes once `daysSinceLine` passes `IndEvo_petLineDays` x `lineRoll` (1 to 1.5, `PetConstants.LINE_DAYS_SPREAD`). Docking (`sayDocking`, rule `IndEvo_petDockingLine` on `PopulateOptions`, `$hasMarket`, `$menuState == main`) shows a line once `daysSinceLine` passes the setting; the main market menu is populated again on every return to it, the cadence keeps it to one. A jump of the player fleet (`reportFleetJumped`, from a pet aboard) and a fight (`reportPlayerEngagement`, from a pet whose ship fought) post a `jump` or `fight` line the same way (`sayMoment`); a fight line comes after `shellShock`, so it reads for a Shell shocked pet. Each line resets both fields. Speaker: a living pet on a ship of the player fleet or in the menagerie, the flagship pet x3 (`FLAGSHIP_LINE_WEIGHT`), a favourite x3 more (`FAVOURITE_LINE_WEIGHT`).
- `status` lines are the card tagline; a stored pet takes only rows with the `storage` context, the others tell of life aboard a ship (`PetDataRepo.Line.weight`); one is picked per pet when the panel opens and kept with its `%s` while it is open, so a rename shows at once. `petting` and `petting_starving` lines answer petting ([Pets panel](#pets-panel)).

## Checks

`indevo/tools/PetChecks.java`, headless, never shipped, not part of `ReleaseBuild`. It reads `pets.csv`, `pet_traits.csv` and `pet_lines.csv` with the `SettingsChecks` CSV parser and checks: UTF-8 without BOM, no curly quotes, cell counts, unique ids; every pet has a class tag, a known body and a known pace, and only `hampter` has the hamster pace; every trait has the `IndEvo_pt_` prefix, a unique name, a known kind and mode, a value, a stat key that `PetHullmod.applyStat` takes (run on stand in ship stats), an effect label, one `%s` in `desc` and `flavour` and no other `%`, and a flavour with no body words; a reduction has a cap of at most 90 and stops there at grade 1000 on a hamster with a nest; every species lean names a talent; over 40,000 seeded bought pets: 1 or 2 talents at grade 1 half and half, unread, every disposition, never Pathfinder, a Salvage Raven a Scavenger more than twice as often; talents sort best first with ties in arrival order, one per heart and all at 5, Pathfinder steps every 4 grades; every line row has a known event, mood, disposition and context, match tokens that are pet ids, bodies or class tags in use, one `%s`, no other `%`, a full stop except on status notes, status notes that start with the name; name rows hold a short capitalized name with no mood, disposition or context; class and `any` lines have no body words; every pet finds a line of every event for every mood and disposition; the pick weights 4, 2, 1.5 and 1 hold over 40,000 seeded picks; heart thresholds rise, one per heart, time reaches the mascot effect but not the nest; in play time (1.5 minutes a week) the first heart takes at most 5 minutes, the third at most 60, the third on Supplies at most 180; one favourite with every want answered at once and no shared events reaches the fourth heart in at most 90 minutes and the fifth in at most 180, a want stays open at least 5 minutes, wants in the fleet come at least 1 minute apart, the cooldown is longer than a want; the pace limits of `PetData.paceFor`; every `PetTitle` has a unique label and a reason that reads as a sentence, with its count where it has one.

```sh
java -cp "out/release-build-<n>/classes:lib/*" indevo.tools.PetChecks
```

Run from the repository root after the compile gate. Use `;` in place of `:` on Windows. It prints `Pets: N checks passed` or every failure and exits 1. It refuses to run inside the game.

## Contracts

- `IndEvo_petDockingLine` is on `PopulateOptions`, a `FireAll` trigger (vanilla `marketDock`: `FireAll PopulateOptions` after `FireBest MarketPostDock`), so it runs next to vanilla's option rows and its line follows the docking text. `MarketPostOpen` is `FireBest` (vanilla `marketOpen` row, `EndConversation.java:54`; the `FireAll` calls in `MarketCMD` and `AbandonMarketPluginImpl` are commented out): a row there competes with `marketPostOpenDefault`, and a win would drop the vanilla row that sets `$tradeMode` and fires `MarketPostDock`.
