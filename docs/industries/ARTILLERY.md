# Artillery

Orbital artillery stations, watchtowers, the campaign projectiles they fire, the derelict defender fight and the player industry. Read it before touching anything in `indevo/industries/artillery/` or the artillery hulls and weapons. It does not cover the combat-side weapon scripts in `data/scripts/weapons` (see [Combat](../core/COMBAT.md)) or the consumables that target or trigger artillery (see [Consumables](../items/CONSUMABLES.md)).

Checked against Starsector 0.98a-RC8 and IE `cbdc2e1`. Recheck engine facts after a game update.

## Overview

Some planets carry the `IndEvo_ArtilleryStationCondition` condition and a station entity (`IndEvo_ArtilleryStation`) of one of three types: `mortar`, `railgun`, `missile`. The station fires campaign projectiles at hostile fleets in range once they are "seen" by a watchtower (`IndEvo_Watchtower`) or a hostile fleet. On uncolonized planets the station is derelict (`IndEvo_derelict` faction), guarded by a defender fleet, and blocks survey and salvage until beaten. After that the planet can be colonized and the station rebuilt as the industry `IndEvo_Artillery_<type>`, which then fires for the owner. Four core-world markets get one at new game.

## Flow

### Two owners for one station

A planet with artillery has one `ArtilleryStationScript` for its whole life. While the market is condition-only it runs `aliveActions` and owns the entity and fleet (`ArtilleryStationScript.java:203-231`). As soon as the market is a real colony, `aliveActions` calls `setDestroyedWithDestroyedActions` and from then on `destroyedActions` runs (`:209-211, 120-145`):

- market has an artillery industry or one in the construction queue (`marketHasArtilleryIndustry`, id match `contains("artillery")` on the lower-cased queue id): clear destroyed flag, remove broken station. The `ArtilleryStation` industry owns a new entity and fleet.
- otherwise: mark condition destroyed, spawn `IndEvo_DestroyedArtilleryStation` (matched to the orbital station orbit, opposite side), disable the plugin, drop the commander.

`ArtilleryStation.isFunctional` also needs another functional industry tagged `station` (`ArtilleryStation.java:84-96`); `isAvailableToBuild` needs the condition, a functional spaceport, an orbital station and `IndEvo_Enable_Artillery` (`:584-597`). `getType()` is the industry id minus `IndEvo_Artillery_` (`:329`).

The tooltip is vanilla `createTooltip`. `addRightAfterDescriptionSection` adds "Missing an orbital station to function!" in every mode but `QUEUED` while no functional station is on the market (`:148-155`); vanilla's own "Currently under construction" line also shows then, because `isFunctional` is false. The CR, ground defense and range lines come from `addPostDemandSection`.

### Station fleet

Both owners build the same station: `FleetFactoryV3` empty patrol fleet, `setStationMode(true)`, hidden, AI null, transponder on, orbiting the entity, one member from the spec JSON `variant` (`ArtilleryStation.java:278-398`, `ArtilleryStationScript.java:421-571`). Not functional or destroyed means "skeleton mode": modules without `vastbulk` are perma-detached and the fleet sits in `STATION_BASE_FLEET` instead of `STATION_FLEET`. Industry CR is `0.5 + 0.5 * min(supply fraction, ship quality)` from metals and heavy machinery demand (`ArtilleryStation.java:131-155`); derelict CR is 1. Alpha core gives a remnant AI commander, otherwise a `tier1StationOfficerLevel` officer.

Station destroyed in battle (`reportFleetDespawnedToListener`): the industry disrupts itself for half its build time and aborts the despawn (`ArtilleryStation.java:466-481`); the derelict script goes destroyed, spawns the broken station and moves the newest battle debris field onto it (`ArtilleryStationScript.java:619-644`).

### Derelict faction tracking

`ArtilleryStationScript.updateFaction` (`:147-187`): the station takes the faction of the first non-player market in the system (skipping `epta_tradebase_market`), else the first non-player station fleet; with neither and the player out of system it reverts to `IndEvo_derelict`. Non-player watchtowers follow. `$IndEvo_isArtilleryActive` on watchtowers is true while the station is derelict or remnant.

### Firing

`CampaignAttackScript.advance` (`:93-110`), only in the player's location. While `IndEvo_Enable_Artillery` is off it removes the range terrain and returns, so placed stations do not fire; the script stays attached and resumes when the setting is back on. Otherwise: add range terrain once, move it along when the station changes location, then if not `disabled`: advance station interval, blocked areas and per-target cooldowns, `updateTargets`, `fireAtTargets`.

- Valid target (`isValid`, `:224-237`): hostile (`isHostileTo`; a null faction is hostile to all but remnants), alive, has AI or is the player, at least `IndEvo_Artillery_minRange` away, anywhere else in the system, `$IndEvo_WasSeenByOtherEntity` set, not `ENTITY_MISSION_IMPORTANT`.
- Per-target cooldown `IndEvo_Artillery_min/maxCooldown`, times `IndEvo_Artillery_cooldownNPCMult` for NPCs; one station-wide delay `min/maxDelayBetweenShots` between shots at different targets. `advanceStationFireInterval` rebuilds the interval when either delay setting changes (`:375-377`), which restarts the current wait.
- Skips: fleet in battle (retry in 10 s), mortar target area blocked by an earlier mortar volley within 400 su, safe spot: fleet or predicted position within `primary radius + 700` of any market primary entity (`isInSafeSpot`, `:335-362`).
- Forced targets (`forceTarget`, `:140-152`) bypass `isValid` and are cleared after one shot. Called by the decoy mine and the ancient remote ([Consumables](../items/CONSUMABLES.md)) and every 5 s on the player in dev mode (`:294-297`).
- Range: no upper limit, the whole system. The range terrain has radius `TERRAIN_RADIUS` (1,000,000 su, `:33`) only so its name, tooltip and safe-spot flash show anywhere in the system; it draws nothing.
- Warning time: the reticule or danger sign always shows for the warning time before impact, at any range. Mortar warning is `IndEvo_Artillery_mortar_projectilesImpactTime` (4 s) plus 0 to 2.4 s per shell (`WARNING_SPREAD`), missile `IndEvo_Artillery_missile_projectilesImpactTime` times 0.8 to 1.2 per carrier. A shell waits `warning - flight time` and leaves at the reticule; a longer flight leaves after a short wind up (mortar `MIN_LAUNCH_DELAY` 0.5 s plus the warning spread, missile 0.3 to 0.7 s) and the reticule shows later (`revealSeconds = impactSeconds - warningSeconds`). The aim leads the target by the whole flight (`MortarShotScript.getLeadSeconds`, `MissileShotScript.getAnticipatedTargetLoc`), so a far target that turns is missed. NPC fleets get the avoid order only once the reticule shows.
- Player shots aim again when the reticule shows (`reveal`): the shell or carrier keeps its scatter offset (`trackOffset`) around the player's position predicted for the remaining time and keeps its impact time. A shell in flight changes speed for that, clamped to 0.25x to 3x (`MIN/MAX_RETARGET_SPEED_MULT`); a carrier lays a new arc from where it is, bent to the same side, split at 70% (`RETARGET_SPLIT_FRACT`), at most 3x speed. NPC targets keep the aim from the moment of firing. The railgun is not affected: nothing flies, its warning to hit time never depends on range.

Volleys:

- Mortar: `MortarShotScript -> MortarProjectileEntityPlugin` x `IndEvo_Artillery_mortar_projectilesPerShot`, random points in a 250 to 700 su circle around the position predicted at impact time. NPC fleets near a shown reticule get `ArtilleryReactionScript -> ModularFleetAIAPI.getNavModule().avoidLocation`.
- Railgun: `RailgunShotEntity -> RailgunProjectileEntityPlugin` x `IndEvo_Artillery_railgun_projectilesPerShot`, alternating lead fuzz `IndEvo_Artillery_railgun_shotFuzzMult`. Shot `i` (from 1) fires at `(IndEvo_Artillery_railgun_projectilesImpactTime + i + random) * LANCE_TIME_MULT` (0.4) seconds, 1.0 to 1.4 s for the first shot and 0.4 s apart at the default 1.5, but leads the target for `LEAD_TIME_MULT` (0.8) times the same sum (`RailgunShotEntity.initProjectiles`), twice the fire time like the old shells: a fleet holding course is passed in front, hits come from the lead fuzz and from speeding up. Nothing flies: the target line holds until that moment, then `fire()` hits the nearest fleet along the line within 50 su of it (`HIT_DISTANCE`), at least `IndEvo_Artillery_minRange` and 30% of the target distance out (`FRIENDLY_FIRE_IMMUNITY_DIST_FRACT`). The beam ends at the hit, or at `MAX_RANGE` (50000 su) on a miss. The fire sound plays at the beam point nearest the sound listener (`Misc.closestPointOnSegmentToPoint` with `SoundPlayerAPI.getListenerPos`), so it fades with the distance to the beam, not to the station. Planets do not block it. The warning to hit time does not depend on range.
- Railgun look: the warning line fades in over the first 10% and holds at `MAX_LINE_ALPHA`, orange to red. The beam (`IndEvo_stream_core` halo and glow, `IndEvo_line` white core, muzzle glow) is full for `BEAM_HOLD` (0.06 s), decays with `BEAM_DECAY` (0.11 s) and is gone after `BEAM_DURATION` (0.4 s). A thin afterglow with moving dashes fades over `AFTERGLOW_DURATION` (1.1 s), then the entity expires.
- Missile: `MissileShotScript` spawns an even number of `MissileCarrierEntityPlugin` on three-point arcs (`TrigHelper`, see [Utils](../core/UTILS.md)); a carrier launches at `impactSeconds - flight time`, but never before 0.3 to 0.7 s (`MIN_LAUNCH_DELAY`, `LAUNCH_DELAY_SPREAD`), so the station wind-up always shows and a long flight lands later, with the danger sign held back until the warning time before impact; at launch `moveStartToStation` lays the arc again from the station's current location through the split point and the target, because the station orbits on while the carrier waits; at 30% of the remaining distance each splits into 3 `MissileSubmunitionEntity` that home on hostiles within 600 su; each ends in `ECMExplosion` (interdict like the interdiction mine, plus `IndEvo_slowfield` terrain for `IndEvo_Artillery_missile_duration` seconds). A carrier that comes within `DETECTION_RADIUS` of a hostile fleet first bursts once into an `ECMExplosion` and does not split.

Shells, carriers, submunitions, lances and every explosion cut the fog weather: `FogTerrainPlugin.feedShot` finds them by plugin class and reads `RailgunProjectileEntityPlugin.isFired()` and `originLocation` ([FOG](../exploration/FOG.md)).

Mortar and railgun explosions use `ExplosionFleetDamage.LOW` (`MortarProjectileEntityPlugin.java:313`, `RailgunProjectileEntityPlugin.fire`).

### Fire animation

Drawing only: no effect on hits, timing or the save. The station sprite never moves or turns; every position is the station's location plus an offset along the shot's angle.

Each projectile entity waits at the station until its own launch time (`projectileDelayTime`; railgun: `impactSeconds`), so a volley leaves shot by shot. While it waits it calls `ArtilleryFireRenderer.reportIncoming(station, type, angle, secondsToShot)` every frame, and `reportShot(station, type, angle)` once, next to its fire sound (`MortarProjectileEntityPlugin.advance`, `RailgunProjectileEntityPlugin.advance`, `MissileCarrierEntityPlugin.advance`). Both return when the station is not in the player's location. The renderer keeps one `StationFireEffect` per station, advances it while the game is not paused, drops it when it is idle and draws it in `TERRAIN_7` (above the station, below fleets) with the station's sensor fade.

| Phase | What `StationFireEffect` draws |
|---|---|
| Wind-up | Glow at the muzzle over the last 0.5 s (mortar), 0.8 s (railgun) or 0.3 s (missile) before a shot (`incoming`) |
| Shot (`shot`) | Flash for 0.13 s (mortar star: one long streak forward, two short ones 54 degrees to the sides, a thin cross flare and a light on the station; railgun: a short faint lance and 16 sparks that fly 20 to 100 su; missile glow) and smoke out of a rear port and two side ports; the missile station puffs at the tube of each missile |
| Heat | Orange glow and rim on the station. Each shot adds `max / shots per volley` (max 1, missile 0.6) |
| Vent | Starts 0.45 s after the last shot once no projectile is waiting, a new shot stops it. Runs for 60% of `IndEvo_Artillery_minCooldown` (at least 4 s, 9 s by default) and cools the glow to 0. Mortar: 5 steam puffs out of the three ports, the glow drops a step with each. Railgun: two fast streams out the back for 2 s that take 75% of the glow. Missile: slow discharge all around the station that turns as it drifts out |

Smoke is the vanilla `nebula_particles` sheet in normal blend, glows are `fusion_lamp_glow`, sparks and the lance are `IndEvo_line`, the mortar streaks and the rim are plain GL. All sizes, counts and times are constants in `StationFireEffect`.

### Seen state and watchtowers

`EyeIndicatorScript.advance` (`:46-138`, every 0.5 s, only in systems tagged `IndEvo_SystemHasArtillery` with a station hostile to the player and `IndEvo_Enable_Artillery` on): any NPC fleet visible to a hostile AI fleet gets seen for 5 days; the player seen by a hostile fleet locks instantly. Range sources fill the eye, the fastest source counts. A hostile unhacked watchtower adds `1 + share` per second up to 20, share being `1 - dist / IndEvo_Artillery_WatchtowerRange`, so 10 to 20 s. A lit searchlight tower adds `SEARCHLIGHT_FILL_MULT` (4) times `SearchlightEntityPlugin.getPlayerRate()`, 2.5 to 5 s. Lighthouse beams (`LighthouseEntityPlugin.getBeamFraction`, `1 - dist / beam length`) and the drone net (`DroneNetScript.getPlayerFraction()`, cones and nest lights) ramp with `getRampRate`: a full eye in `RAMP_MIN_SECONDS` (0.5 s, 0.05 days) at the source up to `RAMP_MAX_SECONDS` (3 s, 0.3 days) at the end of its reach, linear in between; the check runs every 0.5 s, so the fastest lock takes two checks. at full the player gets `$IndEvo_WasSeenByOtherEntity` for `SEEN_DAYS` (0.6 days), renewed while full. Leaving range drains it at `DRAIN_RATE` (1 / 1.2 per second, so a full eye empties in 24 s); at 0 the flag is cleared. A player jump empties the eye and clears the flag (`reportFleetJumped`).

`WatchtowerEntityPlugin.advance` (`:91-122`): about once per 20 s in the player's location marks hostile NPC fleets within `IndEvo_Artillery_WatchtowerRange` as seen for 5 days and shows a range ping. Hacking (vanilla objective hack, rules row `cob_hackPrompt_watchtower`) lasts 30 to 60 days and resets the eye.

Watchtowers are placed per system at each jump point, each gate, one gap per gas giant and one gap in the first star-centred ring (`ArtilleryStationPlacer.placeTowers`), discoverable if the system has no markets. `placeWatchtowers(system, factionId)` is the 4.x entry kept for Lost Sector ([Compatibility](../core/COMPATIBILITY.md#optional-integrations)): it sets `$IndEvo_artilleryVision` to `WATCHTOWER` if unset and places watchtowers. When a watchtower changes hands (`reportObjectiveChangedHands`), after `IndEvo_Watchtower_faction_reset` days it reverts to the nearest artillery faction if that is derelict or remnant (`WatchtowerFactionResetListener.java:32-79`).

### Vision types and lighthouses

Four types: watchtowers, lighthouses, [searchlights](#searchlights) and the [drone net](#drone-net). `ArtilleryStationPlacer.placeVision(system, factionId)` runs once per artillery system and stores the pick in `$IndEvo_artilleryVision`. A system that already has the key or a watchtower is left alone. Derelict (procgen) systems (`IndEvo_derelict`) roll lighthouses, searchlights or the drone net by the `IndEvo_Artillery_visionWeight_*` settings, never plain watchtowers; all weights 0 gives lighthouses. Plain watchtowers are only for core world artillery and player colonies.

Player colony: with a player owned market in the system the new types turn into plain watchtowers. A lighthouse or searchlight tower checks on its vision tick (`WatchtowerEntityPlugin.replacedForPlayerColony`, every 0.5 s while the player is in the system) and `ArtilleryStationPlacer.replaceWithWatchtower` puts a plain `IndEvo_Watchtower` on its orbit, drops the `IndEvo_watchtower` tag from the old tower, fades it out (its beam or lights expire with it) and sets `$IndEvo_artilleryVision` to `WATCHTOWER`. The drone net converts itself (see [Drone net](#drone-net)). No single callback covers both colonizing and taking a market (`PlayerColonizationListener` only reports colonizing), so the tower tick checks the markets.

Lighthouses (`VisionType.LIGHTHOUSE`):

- Placement: `placeLighthouses` uses the watchtower spots, then adds lighthouses on circular orbits around the system centre until there are `MIN_LIGHTHOUSES_PER_SYSTEM` (4), spread between 1500 su outside the star and the outermost planet or jump point (capped at 16000 su).
- Entity: `IndEvo_Lighthouse` carries the `objective` and `IndEvo_watchtower` tags and `LighthouseEntityPlugin extends WatchtowerEntityPlugin`, so hacking, the rules rows, `WatchtowerFactionResetListener` and `WatchtowerStableLocationPatch` treat it as a watchtower. It reuses the `IndEvo_Watchtower` description.
- Lit state (`WatchtowerEntityPlugin.shouldBeLit`, checked every 0.5 s in the player's location): `IndEvo_Enable_Artillery` on, functional, not hacked, faction hostile to the player, no player owned market in the system, and a working artillery station hostile to the player in the system (`ArtilleryStationEntityPlugin.hasWorkingArtilleryHostileToPlayer`: not disrupted, so a beaten derelict station turns the beams off).
- Lit: only the beams see. The player inside a beam fills the eye in 0.5 s at the lighthouse to 3 s at the end of the beam (`getBeamFraction`, see [Seen state](#seen-state-and-watchtowers)). A beam is 20 degrees wide and turns in 225 s, so it sits on a still fleet for about 12.5 s. Hostile NPC fleets inside a beam are marked seen for 5 days (`markFleetsInBeam`). The watchtower range fill and range ping are skipped.
- Unlit: the beams are hidden and see nothing, and the lighthouse runs the plain watchtower code (`super.advanceVision`), so a captured lighthouse still spots for the owner's artillery by range.
- Beam: `LighthouseBeamTerrain` is created by the plugin on its first frame in the player's location and sits on the lighthouse. Each lighthouse rolls its beams once (`LighthouseEntityPlugin.rollBeamCount`: 30% one, 60% two, 10% three, spread evenly, 180 or 120 degrees apart) and hands the count to its terrain. `IndEvo_Artillery_lighthouse_beamLength` (6000 su), `_beamWidth` (20 degrees), `_turnTime` (225 s per turn) are read at use time. The beam length is the max: `getCurrentLength` scales it to 0.8 to 1 by the player's sensor profile ([Profile scale](#profile-scale)), for drawing, the map wedge, `sees` and `getBeamFraction`. Beam count, start angle and turn direction are saved.
- Drawing: `LightConeRenderer.draw` per beam with `LighthouseBeamTerrain.LIGHT` (peak alpha 0.18, 65% in a narrow core, the rest in a halo), then `drawDust` and the lamp: a 700 su spill, a 220 su bloom, an 80 su glow at the root of each beam and a white core. Warm white tinted 20% to the faction colour, no push. Brightness fades over 1 s on lit changes. `renderOnMap` draws each beam as a fading wedge on the system map.
- `LightConeRenderer`: an additive mesh with no outline. An arc of 360 degrees with `edgeStart` 1 draws a round light. Brightness at a point is `Style.peak * Style.across(angle / half arc) * Style.along(distance / drawn length)`. `across` is a halo `1 - smoothstep(edgeStart, edgeEnd)` and an optional core `exp(-(t / coreWidth)^2)` with `coreShare` of the light; `along` is `(farShare + (1 - farShare) / (1 + falloff r^2))` with a fade out from `endStart` and a fade in at the lamp. It draws `edgeEnd` times the checked arc and 1.1 times the checked length, which puts the checked edge inside the fade.
- Light and shadow in the mesh: a blocker takes `Style.shadow` of the light behind it, the rest is drawn as a second, unblocked pass. A ray that hits a planet ends at the planet's middle and fades across its near side; a ray that hits a chaff cloud ends 60 su soft where it enters. Beside a blocker the shadow edge is soft: it starts at the blocker and its width grows with the distance behind it (`Style.sourceRadius`). The dark end of that edge is the checked shadow of `VisionBlockers`. Rays are spread evenly, with extra rays on both sides of every blocker edge and across its near side; every ray has its own sample distances (rings, blocker distances, the end of a blocked ray, the start of a shadow edge) and the strip between two rays is stitched by distance (`drawMesh`). Scratch arrays are static: drawing runs on one thread.
- Dust (`drawDust`, `Style.dustGain`): one point per 50 su cell of the visible part of the beam's reach, placed by a hash of the cell so it stays where it is in space, wandering 14 su. A point shows with `getLight` at its position times `dustGain`, so only inside the light and not in a shadow, at most alpha 0.6. Points are 1 px, 7% are 2 px, at every zoom. `dustGain` is 5 for beams, 2.5 for spotter cones, 3 for nest lights.
- Shadows: `VisionBlockers.collect(location)`, refreshed every 0.1 s, for drawing and for `sees`: a planet lights up to its middle and shadows everything behind it, a chaff cloud (`SmokeCloudTerrain`) stops the light where it enters. Go Dark only shortens beams through the profile scale. This includes the body the lighthouse orbits, so a lighthouse around a gas giant is dark while a beam sweeps over it. The vanilla `RangeBlockerUtil` is not used: it is built for a pulsar and lets a beam run up to 20% of its range past a planet.
- `sees(point, radius, checkSeconds)` widens the arc by the sweep during one check interval, so a fast or thin beam does not skip a fleet between two 0.5 s checks.

### Searchlights

`VisionType.SEARCHLIGHT`: towers at the watchtower spots (`placeTowers` with `IndEvo_Searchlight`), each with six round lights that circle it. Orange lights see moving fleets, purple lights see stopped fleets, so a fleet crossing the lights has to switch between the two.

- Entity: `IndEvo_Searchlight` carries the `objective` and `IndEvo_watchtower` tags and `SearchlightEntityPlugin extends WatchtowerEntityPlugin`, the same way as the [lighthouse](#vision-types-and-lighthouses): hacking, rules rows, faction reset and the stable location patch treat it as a watchtower. Lit state is `WatchtowerEntityPlugin.shouldBeLit`, checked every 0.5 s; unlit the lights fade out over 1 s and the tower runs the plain watchtower code.
- Terrain: `SearchlightTerrain` is created by the plugin on its first frame in the player's location and sits on the tower. All sizes are shares of `IndEvo_Artillery_WatchtowerRange` (4000 su), read at use time: three rings at 0.3125, 0.5875 and 0.825 of the range with two spots each, 180 degrees apart; spot radius 0.1625 of the range (650 su) at most: `getCurrentSpotRadius` scales it to 0.4 to 1 by the player's sensor profile ([Profile scale](#profile-scale)), for drawing, the eye and every sight check. The rings turn at 190, 150 and 170 su/s along the ring, neighbours against each other. Spot types alternate around a ring and between rings (`(ring + index) % 2`), three of each. Ring angles are saved, spot state is not.
- Stopped: `SearchlightTerrain.isStopped`, `fleet.getCurrBurnLevel() <= 2` (`STOPPED_BURN`). The go slow key without steering stops the fleet; go slow with steering and Go Dark move at burn 4 to 5 and count as moving. Go Dark otherwise only shrinks the spots through the profile scale.
- Player: `still` eases between 0 and 1 over 0.6 s (`CROSSFADE_SECONDS`) after the fleet crosses the line. `getStrength(type)` is `smooth(still)` for purple and the rest for orange. The type that does not see the fleet is drawn at 35% halo and 20% core. A spot sees the player while its strength is above 0.5 and the fleet's centre is inside its radius, so a dim light never sees.
- Lock on: a spot that sees the player locks once the eye is full (`advanceSpot`, `EyeIndicatorScript.isPlayerSpotted`); before that it fills the eye and keeps its path. A locked spot stays locked while it sees the player, full eye or not. it turns red (`DroneConeTerrain.LOCK_COLOR`) over 0.4 s, gets 30% brighter, sends out a ring, plays the drone lock sound (3 s cooldown per tower) and follows the fleet at 160 su/s, out to 1.3 times the range from the tower (`TRACK_RANGE_MULT`). 1.2 s after it stops seeing the fleet it lets go and flies back to its path at 260 su/s. Spots are offsets from the tower, so they move with its orbit.
- Eye: `getPlayerRate` is `(2 - dist / radius) * strength` of the best spot the fleet is in, or below 0 with none above 0.02. `EyeIndicatorScript` multiplies it by `SEARCHLIGHT_FILL_MULT` (4), see [Seen state](#seen-state-and-watchtowers). The watchtower range fill and range ping are skipped while lit.
- NPC fleets: every 0.5 s `markFleetsInLights` marks hostile NPC fleets inside a spot of the type that fits them (stopped or moving) as seen for 5 days.
- Chaff: `VisionBlockers.collectSmoke`, refreshed every 0.1 s, for sight and for the halo. A chaff cloud between a spot's middle and a fleet, or around the fleet, hides it. Planets throw no shadow in a spot.
- Drawing (`TERRAIN_7`, below the fleets): per spot a halo through `LightConeRenderer.draw` with a 360 degree arc and `SearchlightTerrain.LIGHT` (peak alpha 0.21, drawn to 1.1 radius) tinted 45% to the spot colour, dust, a core glow of half the radius in the spot colour and a white centre. Two hints toward the tower, both fading out while the spot covers the tower: a rim on the tower side of the spot in the halo tint (`SearchlightRenderer.drawRim`, peak at 0.9 radius, alpha `RIM_ALPHA` 0.24 times the halo, 1.15 rad to each side) and a spike per spot on a band around the tower, cribbed from the vanilla Neutrino Detector ring (`GraviticScanAbility.render`): `clearSpikes`, `addSpike` per spot, `drawSpikes`. The band sits at `SPIKE_RING_RADIUS` (46 su), `SPIKE_BAND` (26 su) thick, streaky, drawn twice with a wobble. A spot pushes it out by 2.5% of its distance to the tower (`SPIKE_DIST_SHARE`, 15 to 80 su), over the spot's angular size from the tower (10 to 50 degrees), with a `(1 - |offset / half|)^2` profile in the spot colour, so a farther light has a longer spike and a locked light a red one. Band and spikes are only drawn within 500 su of the view (`EMITTER_RENDER_RANGE`). The tower lamp glows in the colour that sees the fleet now. Nothing is drawn on the map.
- Tooltip: the terrain shows as "Searchlight" while the fleet is inside a spot of either type.

### Profile scale

`ProfileScale` (`utils/ProfileScale.java`), one transient instance per `SearchlightTerrain` and `LighthouseBeamTerrain`, made in `readResolve` and advanced in the terrain's `advance`.

- Profile: `getEffectiveProfile` is `fleet.getDetectedRangeMod().computeEffective(fleet.getSensorProfile())`, the detected range vanilla shows on the fleet readout. It counts the ships (5 largest profiles, `maxSensorShips`) and every modifier (transponder +1000 flat, Go Dark x0.5, sustained burn, skills). The drone cones use only the modifier, see [Drone net](#drone-net).
- Share: `(profile - MIN_PROFILE) / (FULL_PROFILE - MIN_PROFILE)`, 100 to 1000, clamped to 0 to 1. `getMult(min)` is `min + (1 - min) * share`: searchlight spots `MIN_SPOT_MULT` 0.4, lighthouse beams `MIN_LENGTH_MULT` 0.8. The setting sizes are the max.
- Resize: the same as the drone cones. The share is sampled every `DroneNetScript.CHECK_SECONDS` (0.25 s) and a new target is reached linearly in `CONE_RESIZE_SECONDS` (0.5 s). Abilities ramp their modifier in (Go Dark, transponder), so the size follows that ramp.
- On the first frame and while the player is in another location the share snaps to the player's profile, so a load or a jump in shows the right size without a visible change.
- Only the player's profile counts. NPC fleets are checked against the current size the player sees.

### Fleet lights

`FleetLightRenderer`: hostile fleets carry a searchlight spot out to the range at which they see the player, so the player sees where a fleet locks the eye at once ([Seen state](#seen-state-and-watchtowers)). Visual only, nothing reads it.

- Gate (`isActive`, checked every `DroneNetScript.CHECK_SECONDS`, 0.25 s): `IndEvo_Artillery_fleetLights` on (default on), the player's location is a system tagged `IndEvo_SystemHasArtillery`, `$IndEvo_SystemDisableWatchtowers` unset, no plain watchtowers (`ArtilleryStationPlacer.usesWatchtowers`: `$IndEvo_artilleryVision` is `WATCHTOWER`, or with no key a plain `IndEvo_Watchtower` in the system) and `ArtilleryStationEntityPlugin.needsVisionAgainstPlayer`. So lighthouse, searchlight and drone net systems only, never core world artillery or player colonies.
- Fleets (`isWatcher`): the ones `EyeIndicatorScript` locks on (hostile to the player, with an AI, not a station), only while the player sees them at least as a sensor contact, so no light shows a fleet the player cannot see.
- Radius (`getDetectionRadius`): `fleet.getMaxSensorRangeToDetect(player)` (player profile plus fleet sensor strength with both modifiers, edge to edge). Vanilla shows composition, which is what `Misc.getVisibleFleets` and the eye need, at that range with the player's transponder on and at `detectionRangeDetailsMult` (0.5) of it with the transponder off (`BaseCampaignEntity.getVisibilityLevelTo`, settings `detectionRangeDetails*`). Both fleet radii are added, so the player fleet's centre on the edge is the moment the fleet sees it, the same rule as a searchlight spot. A new radius is sampled every 0.25 s and reached linearly in `DroneNetScript.CONE_RESIZE_SECONDS` (0.5 s), as in [Profile scale](#profile-scale); a new light starts at its radius.
- Sight (`seesPlayer`): `player.getVisibilityLevelTo(fleet)` at composition or better, the `EyeIndicatorScript` check. While it holds the light turns `DroneConeTerrain.LOCK_COLOR` over 0.4 s, gets 30% brighter and sends out the lock ring once per sighting. No sound.
- Drawing (`TERRAIN_7`, below the fleets): the [searchlight](#searchlights) spot with `SearchlightTerrain.LIGHT`, halo tint, dust, core glow and white centre, no rim, no tower spikes, no chaff shadows. Base colour `BASE_COLOR` (`SearchlightTerrain.ORANGE`, the moving spot colour). Lights fade in and out over 1 s. Nothing on the map.
- Polling: no callback covers a fleet entering the location or a change in what a fleet sees, so the renderer scans the location's fleets every 0.25 s. The renderer is transient and drops every light on a location change.

### Drone net

`VisionType.DRONES`: no towers, detection only. `ArtilleryStationPlacer.placeDroneNet` registers one `DroneNetScript` on the system and adds 3 to 6 nests (one per 4000 su of system extent) on circular orbits around the system centre, spread like the extra lighthouses. Nests launch spotter drones, spotters and nests see, the artillery fires. Nothing in the net fights or chases.

- Nest: entity `IndEvo_DroneNest` (no plugin, watchtower placeholder sprite, discoverable in systems without markets). Always on until destroyed. Keeps `SPOTTERS_PER_NEST` (2) spotters in the air and launches a replacement `RELAUNCH_DAYS` (3 days, 30 s of flight) after one is lost.
- Nest lights: every nest sweeps `NEST_LIGHTS` (3) searchlights, `NEST_LIGHT_ARC` (39 degrees) wide and `NEST_LIGHT_LENGTH` (1000 su) long, times the same player profile mult and eased length as the cones (`getNestLightLength`). The lights sit 120 degrees apart around a base angle from the nest id and each swings `NEST_SWING_ARC` (45 degrees) to each side on a sine, one swing there and back in `NEST_SWING_SECONDS` (11.4 s, about 1.1 days), each light a third of a swing behind the last, with a phase per nest from its id (`getNestSweepAngle`; `getNestLightAngle` is where a light points now). Together they pass over the full circle and leave dark gaps that move around the nest: on the swing centre of a light the longest gap is about 4.1 s, so a fleet at burn 13 or faster from 1000 su gets in unseen. A light that sees the player (inside its arc and length with a free line of sight, or the player touching the nest, checked every 0.25 s) reports `1 - dist / length` to the eye, turns red and 25% brighter over 0.6 s, and the first sight plays the same lock ping and ring as a spotter, at most once per `LOCK_PING_COOLDOWN` per nest. It then locks: it leaves the sweep and turns after the player at up to `NEST_TURN_RATE` (45 degrees per second), and its reach grows to `NEST_TRACK_REACH` (1.3) times the length over 0.5 s, drawn and checked the same. A locked light that loses the player turns to the last seen spot; after `LOSE_SECONDS` (3 s) without sight, or when the net stops seeing, it unlocks, shrinks back and turns back into its sweep at the same rate. A fleet close to the nest can outturn a locked light (burn 20 at 300 su is about 75 degrees per second). Hostile NPC fleets in a light are marked seen for 5 days; lights never lock on them. The sweep runs on `nestSweep` (saved, advanced only while the player is in the system); the light angles, lock and alert state (`NestLights`) are transient, so a load puts every light back on its sweep.
- Spotter: entity `IndEvo_SpotterDrone` (no plugin, not interactable, vanilla derelict picket sprite as placeholder), no orbit. `DroneNetScript.move` sets its location and facing every frame in the player's location. It flies along its heading: burn 6 roaming (120 su/s), burn 7 following (140 su/s), speed changes by 90 su/s per second, the heading turns at up to 50 degrees per second with the turn rate eased in and out (`TURN_ACCELERATION`), and it slows into sharp turns. One thin trail per main engine while it moves. It roams between random points within `ROAM_RADIUS` (4000 su) of its nest. Spotters of a destroyed nest fly on and are not replaced; a respawned nest launches its own.
- Cone: 50 degrees wide, `IndEvo_Artillery_drone_coneLength` (1800 su) times the player's detected range modifier clamped to 0.5 to 1.5, so Go Dark shortens every cone and sustained burn lengthens it. A change of length takes `CONE_RESIZE_SECONDS` (0.5 s); the drawn cone and the sight check use the same eased length. The lamp turns on the hull at up to 30 degrees per second. Roaming it sweeps 30 degrees to each side of the heading over 8 s.
- Seeing the player (`look`, every 0.25 s): inside the cone with a free line of sight. When a spotter that is not following starts to see the player (the cone turns red) it plays the vanilla neutrino detector ping as a UI sound (`LOCK_SOUND_ID`, volume x3) and the vanilla `noticed_player` ring on the drone in the lock colour, at most once per `LOCK_PING_COOLDOWN` (3 s). It reports `1 - dist / coneLength` to the eye from the first check, a full eye in 0.5 s at the drone to 3 s at the cone tip (`getRampRate`). After `NOTICE_SECONDS` (0.5 s) of unbroken sight the spotter follows. It only knows the spot where it last saw the player (`lastSeen`, set on every check that sees): it turns hull and lamp to that spot, closes to 45% of the cone length and holds there. It never backs off, so a fleet can close in for an interdiction pulse. After 3 s without sight it flies to the last seen spot and roams again.
- Line of sight (`VisionBlockers`): a planet or star lights up to its middle and shadows everything behind it; a chaff cloud (`SmokeCloudTerrain`) stops the ray where it enters, so a fleet inside or behind one is not seen. Used for cones and nest lights, for the player and for NPC fleets.
- NPC fleets: a fleet hostile to the net's faction inside a cone or nest light is marked seen for 5 days.
- Decoy: a spotter within `DecoyMineAbility.BASE_RANGE` (3000 su) of an `IndEvo_decoy_mine` flies to it and keeps its cone on it while the decoy exists, whatever else it sees.
- Gate: the net only runs in the player's location. It sees only while `ArtilleryStationEntityPlugin.needsVisionAgainstPlayer` holds (`IndEvo_Enable_Artillery` on, no player owned market in the system, a working artillery station hostile to the player) and `$IndEvo_SystemDisableWatchtowers` is unset. Otherwise cones and zones fade out and nothing is seen; the spotters keep roaming.
- Spotter explosions: a spotter explodes when it carries `$missile_bonked` (set by every IE campaign missile near its path or blast, shared with Catch.Release, never rename), or when `DroneNetScript.reportBlast(location, point, radius)` covers it: the interdiction mine (`InterdictionMineAbility.interdictFleetsInRange`), mortar shell and railgun hit blasts and the missile artillery's `ECMExplosion`. A vanilla interdiction pulse within `InterdictionPulseAbility.getRange` of the pulsing fleet does the same. An exploding spotter lights every spotter within `CHAIN_RADIUS` (400 su) after `CHAIN_DELAY` (0.2 s). Harmless `IndEvo_VariableExplosion`, no loot.
- Nest destruction: a nest carrying `$missile_bonked` explodes and leaves a debris field (150 su, 30 days, on the nest's orbit) holding the nest loot (`DroneNetScript.createNestDrops`, shared with the dialog loot): 1 to 4 Relic Components (`MIN_NEST_RELICS`, `MAX_NEST_RELICS`, one roll each in `IndEvo_droneNest`), one value roll of Heavy Machinery (`NEST_MACHINERY_VALUE` 1500, so 5 to 15 units at base price 150) and one of Metals (`NEST_METALS_VALUE` 1200, so 20 to 60 units at base price 30). Vanilla salvage multipliers apply (easy mode gives 1.5x the relic rolls, the salvage value mult scales the machinery and metals). The relic drop sets `unaffectedByExtMode`, so survival mode thins only the machinery and metals. Interdicts, shells and the chain do not hurt nests.
- Nest dialog (`rules.csv` `IndEvo_droneNest_open`, condition `$tag:IndEvo_droneNest`; the spec tag `has_interaction_dialog` makes vanilla open the rules dialog for an entity without a plugin): "Open fire on the nest" runs `IndEvo_DroneNest destroy`, then "Salvage the wreckage" opens the loot screen with the same drop roll and closes the dialog, "Leave" runs `IndEvo_DroneNest debris` and leaves the debris field instead.
- Player colony: with a player owned market in the system `convertToWatchtowers` removes every nest and spotter, sets `$IndEvo_artilleryVision` to `WATCHTOWER`, places watchtowers with `ArtilleryStationPlacer.placeTowers` and ends the script.
- Polling: the vanilla interdiction pulse has no listener and sets nothing on its targets, only the 0.1 day flag on the pulsing fleet (`InterdictionPulseAbility.java:230`), so the script scans the fleets in the player's location every 0.25 s. Decoys carry no tag and add no listener; the script scans the system's custom entities in the same check.
- Drawing: `DroneConeTerrain` reads the script from `$IndEvo_droneNet` and draws one `LightConeRenderer` light per spotter with `DroneConeTerrain.LIGHT` (the lighthouse look: 65% in a narrow core, the rest in a halo, peak alpha 0.13, dust) and a 200 su bloom on the drone (`TERRAIN_7A`, below the fleets, see [lighthouses](#vision-types-and-lighthouses)): warm white, turning red and 25% brighter over 0.6 s while it sees the player. Every nest draws its three lights in the same `LIGHT` look, discovered or not, so the lights show where an undiscovered nest is, red while they see the player, and an 80 su glow on the nest. Above the fleets (`TERRAIN_8`) it draws each spotter's lamp glow, which pulses while it sees the player, and four engine glows that grow with its speed (engine points from the picket hull file, `DroneNetScript.getEnginePoint`); the engine glows show whether the net sees or not. `renderOnMap` draws only the unlocked reach of each discovered nest's lights, as a filled ring; cones and the lights themselves are not on the map. Cones are drawn for every spotter in the system.
- Nest respawn: `destroyNest` records a `NestRespawn` (orbit radius around the system centre, orbit period, time). `NEST_RESPAWN_DAYS` (60 days, about 10 minutes at 1x) later the check adds a new nest through `addNest` on that radius and period at a random angle: two new spotters, discoverable again in a system without markets (`respawnNests`, part of the 0.25 s check, so a respawn that falls due while the player is away happens on the next visit).
- The script ends itself when no nest, no spotter and no pending respawn is left, so destroying every nest does not end a drone net; only the colony conversion does.

### Defender fight

`rules.csv` `IndEvo_ArtilleryInitialStation` (station, condition-only market, not defeated) -> option `IndEvo_ArtilleryCombatInteraction -> IndEvo_SalvageDefenderInteraction` (`:27`). Defenders: existing `$defenderFleet` on the station or `createDefenderFleet` (`IndEvo_ArtilleryDefenderGen.java:81-109`): `PATROL_LARGE`, `IndEvo_Artillery_defense_FP2 * (1 + min(1, (200 - hazard) / 100))` FP, officers x1.5, seeded from the planet salvage seed, plus the station variant. Win: station fleet members removed with flash, script goes destroyed, `$defenderFleetDefeated`, `FireBest BeatDefendersContinue` (`:83-100`). Loss with damage: defenders persist 10 days via `FleetAdvanceScript`. Salvage adds AI cores per destroyed hull size. The `PopulateOptions` rows disable survey, salvage and Nexerelin outpost/deciv options while undefeated.

### Stable locations

Watchtowers carry the `objective` tag (`data/config/custom_entities.json`, entity `IndEvo_Watchtower`) so the comm relay style capture rules apply, but they sit at jump points, gates and gas giant orbits, never on a stable location. Vanilla `Misc.getNumStableLocations` counts every objective (`api/util/Misc.java:5278`), so each watchtower would block one "create stable location" use at the star and inflate the stable location count in the planet search and the intel planet list. `WatchtowerStableLocationPatch` subtracts the system's watchtowers from the result for every caller, vanilla and other mods. Systems without watchtowers are unchanged.

## Contracts

- Off-screen: `CampaignAttackScript`, `ArtilleryStationScript`, `DroneNetScript` and watchtower logic return early outside the player's location, so the off-screen 60-frame throttle (`docs/concepts/campaign-frame.md`) does not apply. Artillery never fires off-screen.
- Tuning settings are read at use time and apply to placed stations: delay between shots, reload, min range, shot counts, impact times, fuzz, explosion sizes, watchtower range and defender FP apply to the next shot or fight.
- `WatchtowerStableLocationPatch` targets `Misc` by API names only (stable). It needs PatchLib (hard dependency); without the patch watchtowers count as stable locations again.
- Save data: `ArtilleryStationScript` (planet script and memory), `CampaignAttackScript` (entity script, target maps keyed by entity id), the industry's `stationFleet`/`stationEntity` fields, `WatchtowerFactionResetListener.watchtowerTimeoutMap`, `EyeIndicatorScript`, each `LighthouseEntityPlugin` (beam reference, lit flag, beam count) and `LighthouseBeamTerrain` (angle, direction, lit flag, beam count), each `SearchlightEntityPlugin` (terrain reference, lit flag) and `SearchlightTerrain` (ring angles, lit flag), each `DroneNetScript` (system script and `$IndEvo_droneNet`: nest and spotter entities, per spotter movement state, the nest light sweep time, pending nest respawns) all persist. The fire animation is not saved: `ArtilleryFireRenderer` is a transient renderer and a load drops heat and smoke. Vision placement is new game only; a save from before lighthouses, searchlights and drone nets keeps its watchtowers. Projectile entities persist while alive.
- The industry and the derelict script must agree on the type: the script stores it in `$IndEvo_ArtilleryType`; the industry derives it from its id. The rebuild options come from the parent `IndEvo_Artillery_base` (`parent_item`).
- `ArtilleryStationScript.getScript(entity)` assumes a non-planet argument orbits the planet (`:79-89`).
- `$IndEvo_ArtilleryStation` is also set as a tag on defender fleets (`IndEvo_ArtilleryDefenderGen.java:107`); `ConsumableItemDropListener` checks that tag (`indevo/items/consumables/listeners/ConsumableItemDropListener.java:48`).
- `RuinsManager.setUpgradeSpec` excludes the rift generator on artillery planets ([Derelicts](DERELICTS.md)).
- `MagicInvisibator` sets alpha 0 and applies 1e6 damage every frame to the deco module so it never fights (`MagicInvisibator.java:20-38`).
- Shot scripts pass a `remote` flag from `ArtilleryRemoteAbilityPlugin.isRemoteTarget` through the projectile params to `VariableExplosionParams` and `ECMExplosion`; mortar and railgun explosions set `artilleryType`. Achievements read both ([ACHIEVEMENTS](../core/ACHIEVEMENTS.md)).
